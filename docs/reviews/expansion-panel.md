# Expansion panel: the Ashfall Shelf, Lowtinder & Tinderwick Bay, the Farlamp Isles (2026-10)

How the plans in [`walkthrough/10-the-farlamp-isles.md`](../world/walkthrough/10-the-farlamp-isles.md)
were scored, argued over and revised **before** building, then scored again **after**. Influences
are described by craft, never by brand.

## The rubric

Score each dimension 0–10, multiply by its weight and divide by 10. The total is out of 100.
**The ship bar is 85**, and any single dimension under 6 blocks shipping.

| # | Dimension | Wt | A 10 looks like | A 4 looks like |
|---|---|--:|---|---|
| 1 | **Exploration & discovery** | 15 | Every screen has something to stop for: a nook, a vista, a secret seen before it's reachable. Off-lane pockets pay. | Lanes between warps, with empty margins. |
| 2 | **Navigation & level design** | 15 | Loops, not corridors. One-way drops and shortcuts home, readable landmarks, a real route with a shape. | Linear, or a maze for its own sake. |
| 3 | **Reward & dopamine** | 15 | Rewards fit the effort and are varied (kin, items, money, a visible change in the world). There's at least one "oh!" moment. | Generic caches only. |
| 4 | **Story & lore fit** | 10 | It answers a question the canon raises and deepens a character or place. It's consistent with `story-bible.md` §11–12. | Flavour text with no tie-in. |
| 5 | **Return motivation** | 10 | Seen early and earned later. More than one reason to come back, and the world says so. | You find it once, by accident. |
| 6 | **Pacing & curve fit** | 10 | Bands sit on the curve (spine §4). Rest points come at sensible intervals. Optional beds don't bend the main-line trend. | Level cliffs, no rest, or a forced grind. |
| 7 | **Creature value** | 10 | Kin you can't get elsewhere, balanced (the sim passes), with sprites that read. | Reused tables from next door. |
| 8 | **Clarity & softlock safety** | 5 | Gates speak. The journal and hints know about it. No flag order strands you. | A silent "no", easy to forget. |
| 9 | **Originality & tone** | 5 | Cosy-melancholy lanterns-in-the-dark. A copy of nothing. | Generic or derivative. |
| 10 | **Cost-to-value (no padding)** | 5 | Every map earns its build cost. | Filler screens. |

- A **Windward vista** from the shelf, with a **Kite crossing** to Windward Stair I (a later shortcut).
- A **hidden slag nook**: seen from the upper ledge, reached by a one-way drop, holding an ash-buried cache.

## Plan 2: Tinderwick South (the reason to return home)

### v1: a Tidecall islet south of the beach with a cache and a rare surf kin

**58.** This is Gullcry Rock again (Originality 4, Story 4, Exploration 5, Navigation 5).
- *Jun:* "A second Gullcry won't pull anyone home."
- *Ossian:* "The canon already has the answer. *The winter the water rose*, forty years ago, drowned the Solarium's stage. It drowned the lower town too."

### v2: **Lowtinder, the drowned old town**

Old Tinderwick stood on the shore until the winter the water rose (story-bible timeline, about
40 years ago). The town moved up the bluff and the old one went under the bay.
- **Seen from minute one:** at low light the roof-ridges and a drowned bell-tower show off the
  Tinderwick beach. An old fisher on the sand says "that's where the town *was*."
- **Opened by Tidecall** (gift 2, about lv 16), right after Pearlmoor: the first reason to go home.

Maps:
- `tinderwick_bay`: a shallow swim route of reef islets, tide-pools and sea-grass, with a swimmer
  trainer or two.
- `lowtinder`: drowned streets on a tidal shelf, rooftops and the old lamp-school.
- The **Bell Cellar**, two floors under the old lamp-house.

The giver is **Grandmother Ottery Tallow**, Brisa's grandmother and Lowtinder's last Lampwarden.
She never left. She lives cosily in the lamp-house's dry upper floor.

Scored **84.5**, just under the bar. Navigation was 8 (a there-and-back) and Reward 8.

### v3 (adopted)
- **Coming round at home.** The Bell Cellar's far exit climbs up **Tinderwick's own old well**,
  a shortcut back into the home town. The rooftops give one-way drops, so the streets loop.
- **The bell comes home.** Raise the old town bell from the cellar (side quest **"The Town
  Bell"**) and it hangs in Tinderwick's square. Tinderwick visibly changes, and ringing it is a
  small ceremony.
- **Three reasons to return:**
  1. **Tidecall** opens the bay (about lv 17–22).
  2. **Brightlight** (the Lamplight tier at 4 Gleams) reveals the lamp-school's moss-garden room,
     where **Mossmantle** (#39, never wild) grows in old lamplight.
  3. **The skiff** (post-game) reaches the bay's outer **deep reef**. Grandmother Tallow asks to
     be rowed out to see the old Tinder Light one more time, then battles you there (ace 66).
- **Embrift** (#14, Ember, never wild) is the Lowtinder rare. It belongs in the Ember town's
  drowned hearths.
- Prizes: a Lumen Drop (cellar B2), a Star-chart, the old lamp-school's purse, and the
  grandmother's gift after the reef battle.

**Debate.**
- *Jun:* "Three letters out of five, scattered across the world, with no list? I'd give up. Tell
  me which shores."
- *Mara:* "Sea maps are where filler hides. Every sea screen needs a stop."
- *Priya:* "The skiff should pay back into the old world. There's deep water off Gullcry,
  Pearlmoor, Dimglass and Hearthfall that nobody can reach yet."

### v2 (adopted)
- **Ione names the coasts.** The journal shows the letters as n/5, with shore hints. Bottles glint.
- **A density rule for sea maps.** Every 15×10 screen carries an islet, wreck, trainer, buoy-cache
  or ambient kin, and `audit_visual` must pass.
- **Deep backtrack.** 4–5 skiff-only caches in existing deep water (Gullcry, Pearlmoor harbour,
  Dimglass II, Hearthfall, Tinderwick reef). The skiff reopens the old world, the way a Gift does.
- The Fogwall turn-round names the fix: "if only you had a glass to read the buoys by…"
