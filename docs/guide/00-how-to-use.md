Welcome, Wayfarer. This guide is written in the old style: a chapter for each stretch of the
journey, a few pages on the places you can easily miss, and some tables to keep beside you.

## How it's organised

- **Getting Started** explains the controls and the basics.
- **Walkthrough** follows the main journey in order. Each chapter starts with what you need
  before you set out.
- **Side Areas & Secrets** covers optional places and why they're worth the detour.
- **After the Dawn** covers everything that opens once the sky is lit again.
- **Reference** has the type chart and the other tables.

## Spoilers

Story chapters carry a spoiler note at the top. If you're only stuck, read the first paragraph of
a section and stop there: each one opens with the answer to "where do I go next?".
