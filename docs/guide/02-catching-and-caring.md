Kin are the creatures of Vesperholm. You start with one and make the rest of your friends along
the road. This chapter covers how you meet them, keep them and help them grow.

## Your first partner

Star-tender Fenn gives you the **vesperlamp** and your first kin at the Vesper Crossroads
waystone, at level 5. You choose one of three:

| Kin | Type | Kindles into | And then |
|-----|------|--------------|----------|
| **Vulpyre**, a hearth-spark fox | Ember | Vulcinder at lv 16 | Blazethorn at lv 34 |
| **Brinix**, a moonlit pooler | Tide | Brindrift at lv 17 | Brinarch at lv 35 |
| **Cloverkit**, a clover sprite | Verdant | Cloverbuck at lv 18 | Cloverhart at lv 36 |

There's no wrong choice. **Brinix** has the gentlest start, because the first Lampwarden keeps
Ember kin and Tide puts out fire. **Cloverkit** is strong against the second Lampwarden's Tide
team. **Vulpyre** has the hardest opening but grows fierce.

## Catching with the vesperlamp

The vesperlamp is a key item, and a plain throw costs nothing. In a battle against a **wild**
kin, choose **LAMP**. You can never catch another trainer's kin.

Your odds improve when:

- **The kin is weak.** Its health bar in the red is the classic moment to throw.
- **It has a trouble on it.** A dozing or chilled kin is much easier to coax in (×2.5). Any other
  trouble (scorched, drenched, numbed and so on) helps a little (×1.5).
- **You use a charge.** A charge is a one-throw booster you spend from the LAMP menu.

A failed throw just wastes the turn. Throw again.

A charge with a condition (water, grass, a type) works as a plain throw if the condition isn't
met, so you don't lose anything by trying.

Two charges are **keepsakes**: Tessaly's Lamp and the Shutter Charm stay in your pack after
every throw, so you can raise them as often as you like.

### Legendary set-pieces

Some rare kin wait at the end of a quest and are met in a special battle. If one gets away
(you knock it out or run), it **withdraws** for a while: win a number of battles elsewhere and
it comes back. Nobody ever stops you trying again. The game's description of the spot tells
you how many wins are left.

## Nicknames

When you catch a kin, or someone gives you one, you're offered the chance to name it. That name
is then kept. If you want to change it later, **Ottilie the Namewright** at the Wickstead
Lamp-school will rename any of your kin.

## The Hearth

You can carry six kin in your lamp. Everyone else rests in **the Hearth**. If you catch a kin
with a full party, it goes straight to the Hearth: nothing is ever lost. Open **HEARTH** from the
pause menu to swap kin in and out. You always keep at least one kin with you.

## Moves and Star-charts

A kin knows up to **four moves**. As it levels up it offers to learn new ones; pick a move to
forget, or keep the old set.

- **Star-charts** teach a move outside the level-up list. Each is used once, from ITEMS, on a kin
  whose type (or the plain figure) suits it. Shops sell the common ones; the strongest are found.
- **Forgot something?** Hesper Vane, who visits the inns, can help a kin remember a forgotten
  move for 250 wicks. She only charges if it works.

## Kindling

When a kin is ready to **kindle** (change into its next form), you get a small ceremony under
the night sky. Press B at any point before the bloom to hold it back; it'll offer again at the
next level. Most kin kindle at a level. A few kindle through **bond** instead.

### Bond

Bond is how close a kin is to you. It never changes battle maths, but some kindlings need it
(Hearthkit is the first you're likely to meet).

- Every battle you **win**, each kin that's still standing gains a little bond.
- Healing a kin with a balm from ITEMS gains a little more.

## Healing and the troubles

There are seven troubles (status conditions): **Scorch, Drench, Numb, Doze, Blight, Dazzle,
Chill**. A rest cures all of them.

- **Rest** at an inn, a home, a fireside or a camp. Resting is always free and heals everything.
  Each rest place also becomes your new wake-up spot if you faint.
- **Balms** (Tallow, Warm, Bright) restore health in or out of battle.
- **A Hearth Coal** is the only item that rouses a fainted kin (at half health). Otherwise, rest.
- **A Lumen Drop** raises a kin one whole level. You can't buy them; they're found in caches
  and given as rewards.

Out of battle, **HEAL ALL** in the ITEMS medicine list spends your cheapest balms first to top up the party.
