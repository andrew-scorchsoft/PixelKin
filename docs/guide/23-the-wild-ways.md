The Wild Ways are four optional places you can see (or hear rumours of) long before you can
open them. Each one opens from somewhere else in the world, and each is a place you *traverse*:
slide, ride, climb and switch your way across.

| Place | Opens from | When | Wild levels | The big prize |
|-------|-----------|------|-------------|---------------|
| The Wick-Run | Sunvault Climb (top) | After the Solar Gleam | 46–50 | Rook's ribbon, run prizes, Chandrek |
| The Rimeway | Pale Vault Glacier | As soon as you reach the Vault | 39–42 | The Frostglass Lens, Vaultclaw |
| The Mirelights | Saltreach Fen (east edge) | Once you hold the Frostglass Lens | 38–43 | **Lanthorne** (lv 45) |
| The Old Seams | Cinderhead Mine | After the Stone Gleam | 27–33 | **Emberroot** (lv 46) |

## Getting around on the Wild Ways

New kinds of ground appear here:

- **Slopes** carry you downhill every step. You can steer left and right, but never walk back up.
- **Glare ice** slides you until something stops you. Look for the stopper rocks.
- **Ore-cart rails** carry you along without steering. Levers switch the junctions.
- **Ladders, rope-planks and trestles** can only be walked along their length. Some trestles let
  you pass underneath them on the way back.
- **Ramps** launch a rider over gaps and rocks. On foot they're just ground.

## The Wick-Run

**The downhill run from the Sunvault Climb to Lightkeeper's Point.** Before the Long Dusk, wax
for Vesperholm's wicks was carted down this chute from the mountain chandleries to Pearlmoor's
quays. Now it's a sport.

**From the bottom (early):** the beach west of Lightkeeper's Point runs on to the **Wick-Run
Foot**. You can walk in from Pearlmoor at any time, and there are no wild kin there. The cable
car (the **Tallow Lift**) is chained at the top, so the only way up is the long way round.

**From the top (after the Solar Gleam):** at **Wick-Run Crest**, off the foot of the Sunvault
Climb, **Pell** rents you a **runner** (a two-wheeled bike) for 300 wicks. Then it's six maps
downhill: chutes, a slalom, a spiral trestle you cross over and then pass under, a rock garden, a
ramp line and the long straight. Reaching the crest also frees the Tallow Lift, so it runs both
ways from then on.

**The time trial.** A clock runs from the start gate to the finish arch.

| Tier | Time | First-time prize | Repeat runs |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|
| Gold | 26.0 s or less | 600 wicks + Rook's **Runner's Ribbon** | 250 wicks every time |
| Silver | 32.5 s or less | 400 wicks | 100 wicks every time |
| Bronze | 41.5 s or less | 200 wicks | — |

The Runner's Ribbon means Pell lends you runners for free. **Beat all the Runners first:** a
battle mid-run counts against the clock. Rook's record is 24 seconds.

**Finds:** a Lumen Drop on a ledge only a ramp reaches, a Beacon Charge in the wax-cart wreck, a
300-wick purse, and 2 Wax Cakes on a ramp-only shelf. Wild kin: Tallowcrest and Wickmere (common),
Solvyne, Dawnfawn and Helibud, the rare **Warmantis**, and the very rare **Chandrek**. There are
rests at both the crest and the foot.

## The Rimeway

**The old star-tenders' high road, north out of Pale Vault Glacier.** Climb glacier walls on
ladders, solve **the Glare** (an ice-slide puzzle, five slides at best, and you can't get stuck),
and reach **the Fold**, a high plateau with the frozen falls and the tenders' refuge (a rest).

| Trainer | Where | Team | Purse |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| Rime-walker Ivo | The ascent | Geodrake 40, Blizzrhare 41 | 656 |
| Rime-walker Sef | The Fold | Geolace 41, Crystarn 41, Vaultclaw 42 | 672 |
| Fog-walker Dunne | The descent | Wispwane 47, Glacitern 47, Stillwarden 48 | 768 |

### "A True-Eye"

**Isel**, a lens-grinder beside Pale Vault's north road, wants to cut a lens that shows a false
light for what it is. She needs clear ice. Slide the plunge pool below the **frozen falls** on the
Fold and take a core of ice from their foot. Bring it back and she cuts the **Frostglass Lens**,
the key to the Mirelights.

### "The Rime Gate"

At the Fold's north end, the **Rime Gate** is barred from the far side. You can see the fog
through the grille, but you can't pass yet. Later, with **Emberward**, come up from the other side
(the deep Coldfog Marches, behind Nightreach) and lift the bar. From then on the Rimeway is a
quick road between the Coldfog and Pale Vault. Hale, the last rime-tender, keeps one lamp
burning at the Fold.

**Vaultclaw** (Frost/Stone) lives only on the Fold.

## The Mirelights

**East of Saltreach Fen, over the Glassmere.** From your first trip through the fen you'll have
seen blue lights flickering over the water east of the ferry stage. The **eel-wife** asks you to
look for her boy **Tobin**, who rowed out after them. **Old Ansel** the ferryman won't cross:
half those lights are lies. Show him the **Frostglass Lens** and he takes you over.

- **Through the Lens, the truth shows.** Every plank walk across the mere ends at an identical
  blue light. Look at the reed at the walk's **mouth**: **lit means sound boards, dead means a
  lie**. A false walk dumps you in the water and back at the start.
- **Currents** pull swimmers. One carries you to a diver's islet with the **Glassmere Pearl**
  (worth 1,200 wicks); a long true walk brings you back.
- **The drowned chapel.** Cross over a trestle, climb down a ladder, and pass back under the
  trestle to find Tobin, lost and embarrassed (he gives you 2 Beacon Charges and goes home). A
  Hollowing acolyte keeps a camp here, which is a rest.

|---------|------|-------|

| Lamp-diver Odo | Lumplass 40, Fernlance 41, Tidwraith 42 | 672 |

Wild kin (38–43) include the Mirelights' own **Tidwraith** and **Lumplass**, and the rare
**Lampling**.

**Lanthorne.** Behind the chapel, the fen's own lamp sleeps inside a null-lantern. Through the
Lens, a true light gets through. **Lanthorne** (Tide/Light, lv 45) wakes. It rests for 12 won
battles if it gets away. Storm and Verdant hit its Tide half; Dark hits its Light half.

## The Old Seams

**Below Cinderhead Mine: "the seam we don't work".** After the Stone Gleam, Otho waits by a sealed
door in the mine's north workings and finally lifts the bar. The old foremen sealed it because the
coal down there is warm, and something is breathing on it.

| Floor | What you do | Wild levels |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| The Shaft Benches | Planks over open pits, zig-zag ladders, a one-way scree spill | 27–29 |
| The Rail Yard | **The lever puzzle** (below) | 29–31 |
| The Scree Galleries | Two one-way scree slopes with a ladder home after each; the foremen's camp (a rest) | 31–33 |
| Emberroot's Heart | The first star that ever fell | — |

**The Rail Yard.** Four platforms round a sunken working, joined by ore-cart rails you ride
without steering. Three **levers** switch three junctions. One lever sits on a siding you can only
reach by riding the rails there, so ride, throw, and ride again. Every line ends on a platform
whatever the levers say, so you can't get stuck: experiment.

|---------|-------|------|-------|
| Seam-miner Kester | Benches | Gravelo 29, Orebound 30 | 480 |
| Seam-miner Wenna | Rail Yard | Pyrolith 30, Riddlestone 31 | 496 |
| Seam-miner Tam | Rail Yard | Sparkrat 30, Orebound 31 | 496 |
| Seam-miner Penhal | Scree | Coalump 31, Lithonyx 32, Pyrolith 33 | 528 |
| Seam-miner Jory | Scree | Orebound 32, Ferrolith 33 | 528 |

**Steer hard left at the top of the west scree** for a ledge only a rider reaches: a **Lumen Drop**.
Wild kin include **Orebound**, **Pyrolith**, **Lithonyx**, the rare **Ferrolith** and the very rare
**Prismara**.

**Emberroot.** The foremen's last ladder down only shows itself to a bright lamp: you need
**Starlight**, which comes with your sixth Gleam. Below, in the root-rock, lies the first star that
ever fell, and at its foot **Emberroot** (Stone/Ember, lv 46). It rests for 12 won battles if it
gets away. Tide hits it four times over. The Foreman's Ledger's last page is pinned nearby.

## The Sunvault Climb

The climb to Nightreach is itself a Wild Way: four stages of ladders, sun-vine bridges, a trestle
over a gully and rope-planks on the Sun Ledges. It's on the main road; see
[[the-west|Chapter 5]].
