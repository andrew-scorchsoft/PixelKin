You'll rarely need to grind for money if you battle the trainers you pass and do the side quests
you find. When a run of bad luck empties your purse, though, there are honest ways to earn it
back.

## Where wicks come from

| Source | Roughly | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Trainers | Their purse, once each | The backbone. Wild kin pay nothing. |
| Side quests | 150 to 1,500 | Most named quests end with a purse or a good item. |
| Wick-purses and caches | 80 to 2,000 | Hidden along the roads; the early roads each have one. |
| Valuables | 250 to 1,500 each | Sell them at any counter (see [[shops-and-items|Shops, Items & Prices]]). |
| Selling items | Half the price | Any priced item. |
| Sparring and the Wick-Run | See below | The two repeatable earners. |

## Trainer purses

A trainer's purse depends on what kind of trainer they are and how strong their best kin is:

| Trainer class | Pays per level of their ace | Example |
|---------------|----------------------------:|---------|
| Route trainers (wayfarers, net-hands, runners) | 16 | An ace at level 10 pays 160 |
| Keepers (wick-tenders, bloom-wardens, vigil miners) | 20 | An ace at level 21 pays 420 |
| Wren | 24 | Wren's rematch, ace 62, pays 1,488 |
| Lampwardens | 60 | Otho Grist, ace 28, pays 1,680 |
| Vigilants | 80 | Warden Mer, ace 68, pays 5,440 |
| Warden Còr, and Fenn's Last Lesson | 120 | Fenn, ace 70, pays 8,400 |

## Sparring at the Lumenaries

Every Lumenary keeps a **hall apprentice** who'll spar with you once that hall's Gleam is lit, and
pays a keeper's purse **every time you win**.

| Hall | Apprentice | Team | Purse |
|------|------------|------|------:|
| Tinderwick | Wick-tender Rosie Wickers | Wickmoth 8, Tallowpup 8, Hearthkit 9 | 180 |
| Pearlmoor Quay | Net-hand Tomas Sprat | Brinelet 13, Brineroll 14, Shimmral 15 | 300 |
| Lowleaf Hollow | Moss-tender Hazel Alder | Sporeling 19, Dewling 20, Lumournis 21 | 420 |
| Cinderhead Mine | Seam-miner Cobb Collier | Rubbol 25, Voltcrag 26, Ferrolith 27 | 540 |
| Galehigh Terraces | Kite-hand Kestrel Towe | Thrumble 31, Flintbeak 32, Strikeaven 33 | 660 |
| Pale Vault Glacier | Frost-ward Inga Snell | Glaceling 37, Hushbore 38, Prismantus 39 | 780 |
| Sunken Solarium | Sun-keeper Lio Brass | Helibud 43, Goldmane 44, Solreach 45 | 900 |
| Nightreach Observatory | Star-watcher Mab Holloway | Drowshorn 49, Nightwraith 50, Lunarbel 51 | 1,020 |

**The practice bell.** All the halls share one practice bell. After any sparring bout, **every**
apprentice rests until you've won **three battles** somewhere else (wild battles and catches count). So you
can't just hop from hall to hall; it works out at about one bout per four wins. A few post-game
rematches (Gran Tallow, Bram Fathom and the Undertide's Ondine) share the same rest.

**Tip:** spar at the newest hall you've earned. It pays best, and the experience is good too.

## The Wick-Run day purse

Once you've earned a tier's one-time prize on the Wick-Run (see [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]]),
every repeat run at that tier pays a **day purse**:

| Tier | Time | Repeat purse |
|------|------|-------------:|
| Gold | 26 s or less | 250 |
| Silver | 32.5 s or less | 100 |

The **Tallow Lift** carries you back up from the foot, so a lap takes about two minutes. Rentals
cost 300 wicks until you win gold, so silver laps only make money once you hold Rook's Runner's
Ribbon (free rentals).

## After the dawn

Post-game money is plentiful by design. The big earners:

- **The Starfall Vigils**: each Vigilant pays around 5,000 wicks, the Stormfall Vigil's reward is
  5,000 more, and Fenn's Last Lesson pays 8,400.
- **Re-runnable bouts**: Wren in Dawnstead (1,488), and every Vigilant and Fenn once the Vigils are
  done.
- **The Farlamp Isles**: two 2,000-wick purses, the sunken *Heron*'s 800, boat-trainers paying over
  1,000 each, and Bram Fathom (5,600, then 5,760 on rematch).

## When you lose

A blackout costs a small, capped tithe, softer each time you lose to the same foe, and never takes
you below 300 wicks. The details are in [[battles-and-wicks|Battles, Wicks & Fainting]]. With
**Cannot die** turned on in Settings, it costs nothing at all.

## Spending wisely

- **Hearth Coals** (1,500) are expensive insurance. Carry one or two for Lampwarden fights; use a
  rest otherwise.
- **Beacon Charges** (600) are worth it for a rare kin; for everyday catches, weaken the kin and
  use a plain throw.
- **Star-charts** are the big aspirational spend. A strong move of your kin's own type can turn a
  hard Lampwarden fight.
- **The Hooded Lamp** (600) pays for itself in time if you're crossing grass you've already
  cleared.
