## How damage works

| Matchup | Damage |
|---------|--------|
| Super-effective | ×2 |
| Neutral | ×1 |
| Resisted | ×½ |
| No effect | ×0 |

- **Two types multiply.** A move that's super-effective against both of a kin's types does four
  times the damage; one that's super-effective against one and resisted by the other breaks even.
- **Same type, more power.** A move that matches its user's own type hits harder.

## The ones that catch people out

- **Storm moves do nothing to Stone kin.** Not "a little": nothing. This matters at Mira's
  Lumenary (bring Stone) and in reverse at Otho's (don't bring Storm attacks).
- **Lunar moves do nothing to Dark kin.** This is why a Dark kin is the best answer to Nessa, and
  why Lunar attackers are no use against the Hollowing.
- **The two mirror axes.** Solar and Lunar hit each other super-effectively, and so do Light and
  Dark. Whoever moves first tends to win those fights.
- **Frost is a great attacker and a poor defender.** It hits Verdant, Storm and Stone hard, but
  Ember and Solar both melt it.
