From the first time you stand on Lightkeeper's Point, a bank of fog sits on the southern horizon
with a lamp blinking inside it. That's the **Fogwall**, and behind it lie the **Farlamp Isles**.
The night the Long Dusk fell, the deep came up: the sea rose a fathom, and the **Farlamp**, the
outermost sea-light, drowned still burning. The isles' town, **Driftholm**, has kept small lamps
alone for eleven winters, and thrown letters into the sea.

When you relight the Keystar the mainland gets its morning, but the Fogwall keeps its own dusk.

## Before the dawn: the driftletters

**Ione Fathom**, an old boatwright mending a hull under the tower on Lightkeeper's Point, tells you
the far lamp went quiet eleven winters back. Five **driftletters** (bottles with letters inside)
wash up on five shores, and you can collect them any time:

| # | Shore |
|---|-------|
| 1 | The Dimglass strand, on the first half of the coast road |
| 2 | Gullcry Rock (Tidecall) |
| 3 | The beach at the Wick-Run Foot, west of Lightkeeper's Point |
| 4 | The Glassmere shallows, in the Mirelights |
| 5 | The shingle under the Drownlight Beacon, in the Coldfog Marches |

The JOURNAL's **"The Farlamp"** entry counts them and names the shores.

## The unlock chain

**Where next? Ione on Lightkeeper's Point, with three driftletters, after the dawn.**

1. **Ione.** With the sky lit everywhere but there, and three letters in your bag, she knows
   Driftholm is alive. She fetches the old lamp-boat's hull and asks for its prow-lamp to be lit
   from the Star-tender's light.
2. **Fenn**, on the front at Dawnstead, lights the prow-lamp.
3. **Back to Ione.** She hands you the **Farlamp Skiff**. You launch from the slip below the tower
   (there's a second slip at the Wick-Run Foot).

**The skiff** lets you row the open **deep water**, past the two-tile shallow shelf that Tidecall
can swim. Deep water has its own wild kin. The skiff isn't a Lantern Gift: no Lampwarden could
have given it.

**Deep-water caches** on the mainland open too: off Gullcry Rock (a Lumen Drop and 2 Bright Balms),
off the Dimglass flats (a Lumen Drop and a Moth-amber), in Pearlmoor harbour (a Starglass Shard and
a Hearth Coal), at Hearthfall, and on Tinderwick Bay's reef. Gran Tallow in Lowtinder also has a
favour to ask (see [[lowtinder|Lowtinder & Tinderwick Bay]]).

**The Fogwall turns you round.** Row into its heart without help and the fog spins you back to its
edge. You need the old keepers' **fog-glass** to read the buoys through it.

**The isles' kin.** **Fathomere** (Tide/Light) and **Beaconclaw** (Stone/Light) are the common
sea-kin. Their young forms, **Fathomote** (kindles at 40) and **Hermiwick** (at 42), are the rarer
finds. **Fogwisp** (Frost/Dark) haunts the fog.

## The Undertide

**Where next? Into the sea-caves under Gullwrack and Kelpcrown, for the fog-glass.**

The Undertide is a sea-cave maze across four floors. Its paths are **tide-races**: calm lanes of
current that carry you along (you can't steer). **Tide-bells** switch the junctions: ring a bell and
the races change direction. Every race ends somewhere safe whatever the bells say, so experiment.

| Floor | Entrance | What's there |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| I, the mouth | Gullwrack Isle | The lesson: one bell, one junction. Lamp-diver Ness (Fathomote 60, Glostern 61, Fathomere 62). A Hearth Coal. Keeper's log page 1. |
| II, the Kelp Vaults | Kelpcrown Isle, or the ladder from I | **The fog-glass**, on an island you can see from the stair: ride to the bell vault, ring the bell, ride again. A Lumen Drop; Lamp-diver Corin (Hermiwick 61, Brinewrath 62, Tidalarch 63). Page 2. |
| III, the Bell Galleries | Driftholm's well-stair | Three bells: Deep, Shoal and Harbour. Net-hand Bryn and Wick-tender Isolde. The **keeper's ledge** (a rest). Page 3. |
| IV, the Drowned Stair | Ladder from III | Races running down, terrace to terrace, and one long up-race home. A Lumen Drop, a Hearth Coal, the **keeper's 2,000-wick purse**, Lamp-diver Marlo, and a dry niche to rest in before the lamp. Page 4. |

**The fog-glass** reads the buoys: the eight lantern-buoys in the Fogwall light up, and you can
follow them south to Driftholm.

## Driftholm

A town that hasn't seen a boat in eleven winters. **The Lamplighters' Rest** is the inn,
**Creel & Daughter** is the chandlery (Warm Balms, Bright Balms, Hearth Coals and charges), and the
**lamp-stone** in the square is a TRAVEL waystone.

- **Elder Nan Creel** keeps the key to the **well-stair** in the square. It goes down under the sea
  to the Bell Galleries, and on to the drowned Farlamp.
- **"Letters Home."** Five of the driftletters were written here. Bring each to its writer:
  **Marra** by the kelp-racks, **Lorne** at the lamp-stone, **Maudie** the schoolteacher, **Hesk**
  the net-mender, and **Elder Creel** herself. With all five home, she gives you **3 Deep Charms**
  and a **Lumen Drop**.

## The Drowned Lamp

**Where next? Down Elder Creel's well-stair, through the Bell Galleries and the Drowned Stair, to
the Farlamp. Light it.**

The Bell Galleries need the **Deep** and **Shoal** bells rung to open the way down. The Drowned
Stair runs downhill to the lamp's air pocket at the bottom of the sea.

Raise your vesperlamp, still carrying the prow-flame Fenn lit from the Keystar, and **relight the
Farlamp**. The fog lifts, and the isles get their own dawn. (It isn't a Gleam; it isn't that kind of
light.)

Then **Farlume** (Tide/Light, lv 68), the drowned lamp's guardian, rises. It's a catch, and it rests
for 12 won battles if it gets away. Storm and Verdant hit its Tide half; Dark hits its Light half.

**After the isles' dawn:** the tide-lamps along every race light up, the wild tables change (the
kindled forms lead), **Keeper Edda Marrow** waits by the lit lamp, and **Ondine** the diver waits at
the top of the Drowned Stair for a repeatable bout. Very deep in the Drowned Stair's pools, the
very rare **Pharolux** (the Glostern line's last form) can appear.

## Bram Fathom

**Where next? Driftholm's quay.** Ione's brother **Bram Fathom**, the isles' best, waits for the
isles' dawn and then asks for a battle.

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Brinewrath | 68 | Tide |
| Stillwarden | 68 | Frost/Dark |
| Tidalarch | 69 | Verdant/Tide |
| Glostrael | 69 | Tide/Light |
| Fathomere | 69 | Tide/Light |
| **Beaconclaw** (ace) | 70 | Stone/Light |

Purse: 5,600 wicks. His rematch (ace 72, 5,760 wicks) shares the halls' practice rest.

- **Bring:** Storm and Verdant for the Tide kin (Storm can't touch Beaconclaw, though; use Tide or
  Verdant there), and Ember or Solar for Stillwarden.

## Side quests on the isles

- **"The Keeper's Log."** Keeper Edda Marrow's last log is in four sea-chests down the Undertide,
  one page per floor. Read all four and a sluice by the Drowned Lamp opens on her **store**, holding
  the **Star-chart: Maelstrom**.
- **"Tide-bell Tuning."** Driftholm's bell-ringers hung the Bell Galleries' three bells to tell the
  tide's order. Ring **Deep, then Shoal, then Harbour**, with nothing rung in between. Racing home
  from the Deep bell passes the other two in the right order. Reward: the bell-ringers' tin (a
  **Lumen Drop**) and a shortcut race from the stair gallery straight back to the well-stair. It
  also wakes a race to an islet on the first floor with a Starglass Shard.
- **The Deep Charm** (×3.0 catch on kin met over the open deep) goes on sale at Creel & Daughter
  after the isles' dawn, 900 wicks each.
