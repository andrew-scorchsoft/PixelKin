## Dimglass Coast II, the tidal flats

**Where next? North past the coast-keeper and across the flats to Pearlmoor Quay.**

With the Ember Gleam in your sky, the coast-keeper waves you on. The second half of the coast is
wide tidal flats under the lantern-buoys. A dry trail runs the whole way, so the dune grass is
optional.

- **The cockler** sits by a driftwood fire just past the start of the flats. Talk to her for a
  full rest. It's the only rest between Tinderwick and Pearlmoor, and it becomes your wake-up
  spot if you faint out here.
- **Fenn** is out on the flats, watching the sky. He explains what the vanishing star means:
  stars and kin hold hands in Vesperholm, and a snuffed light takes a star with it. If you carry
  **Gran's letter**, give it to him here.
- **Caches** on the flats hold 2 Tallow Balms, a Glow Charge and a couple of small purses.

**Remember this spot.** Two side paths branch off here: the buoy line out to **Gullcry Rock**
(needs Tidecall, which you'll earn this chapter) and the dark mouth of **Tideglass Cavern**
(needs Glimmerstep, from the next region).

## Pearlmoor Quay

**Where next? Up through the town to the Lumenary at the very top, and talk to Reyl Wash.**

Pearlmoor is a moonlit fishing port of wet boardwalks and lantern-strings. Things to know:

- **The inn** is a free rest. Rest before every big fight here.
- **The chandlery** sells Warm Balms and the first **Hearth Coals** (the only item that revives a
  fainted kin), plus Star-charts.
- **The breakwater** runs out to sea from the south end of the quay. Its gate is chained for now.
  You can see the silent **moor-bell** shrine at its far end.

Reyl Wash keeps the Tide, and he won't test your bond yet. The Tide-blessing can't start until
the moor-bell rings, and its rope was lost in a storm. He also has a house rule: nobody fights
him until they've **laughed**. He sends you to an old lighthouse on the Point.

## The Causeway Bell

This errand has four steps. The JOURNAL tracks it as **"The Causeway Bell"**.

### 1. Climb the Old Light

**Where next? Follow the beach path west off the quay, past the signpost, to Lightkeeper's
Point, and climb the Old Light.**

The Old Light is a seven-floor tower run by a clockwork joke-machine called **Mr. Punchwheel**.
Each floor has a riddle (or a joke) that opens the stairs, and wild kin roam the halls. There's a
tea-urn rest on the third floor, and more rests higher up. At the top, the machine's headline
act stands between you and a card on the drum: **Smiler 14, McClownface 14, JesterHalf 15**.
Beat them, read the card, and you come away with the **netmender's floats**.

The tower has its own floor-by-floor chapter, with every riddle answer:
[[the-old-light|The Old Light, Floor by Floor]].

**This is also the best training ground before Reyl.** The tower's wild kin run from level 9 to
14, and its **Snoozlet** and **Spirlet** are Lunar type, which is super-effective against Reyl's
Tide team.

### 2. The netmender's rope

**Where next? Take the floats to the netmender on Pearlmoor Quay.**

She's had the bell-rope spliced and waiting for years. She hands you the **Moor-bell Rope** and
unchains the moor-gate at the south end of the quay. You don't even need to find her: head down
the causeway towards the moor-gate with the floats and she'll put down her nets and come to you.

### 3. Ring the moor-bell

**Where next? Out along the breakwater to the end, and ring the moor-bell.**

Two bored net-hands work the causeway, posted on narrow chokes you can't slip past. There's a
cache with a Hearth Coal and a Warm Balm on the way.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Net-hand Maren | Brinelet 12, Lumpin 12 | 192 |
| Net-hand Cob | Lumpin 13, Brineroll 14 | 224 |

Hang the rope and ring the bell. The whole quay hears it.

### 4. Reyl Wash

**Where next? Rest at the inn, then face Reyl at his altar in the Lumenary.**

### Lampwarden Reyl Wash (Tide)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Brinelet | 12 | Tide |
| Lumpin | 13 | Tide/Light |
| Brineroll | 14 | Tide |
| **Shimmral** (ace) | 16 | Tide/Light |

Purse: 960 wicks.

- **Bring:** Verdant, Storm or Lunar kin. Cloverkit (or Cloverbuck) shines here. If you chose
  Vulpyre, catch a Snoozlet or Spirlet in the Old Light.
- **Avoid:** relying on Ember; Tide puts it out.
- **Level:** 13–14 is enough if your types are right.

Win and Reyl grants the **Tide Gleam** and the Lantern Gift **Tidecall**. Walk back onto the quay
for the **Tide-blessing**, the port's festival. With both South Gleams lit, the southern quarter
of the Skyweave Crown is whole.

## Tidecall: what just opened

**Tidecall** lets you cross shallow night-water. You swim it: walk into the water and go.

- **Gullcry Rock** (back on the Dimglass flats, out along the buoys): the rare harbour-light kin
  **Glostern** in the surf, and the **Tide Charm** (a ×2.0 catch charge) at the high stone.
- **Pearlmoor harbour:** swim the shallows for Brinix, Brineroll, Shimmral and Tidmote
  (levels 10–12).
- **"The Last Buoy Out"** (netmender, after the Gleam): relight three storm-dark buoys in the
  Dimglass water, **in order, starting nearest the quay and working outward**. Reward: the
  **Drift Charm**, a ×3.0 catch charge for kin met in water.
- **Home has a secret.** Off Tinderwick's beach, past the drowned bell-tower, the sea now lets
  you through. See [[lowtinder|Lowtinder & Tinderwick Bay]].
- **The Hooded Lamp** goes on sale at the Pearlmoor chandlery (600 wicks). Toggle it in ITEMS to
  halve wild encounters while you walk.

**Later:** after the bell rings, the **old fisher** in Pearlmoor's inn tells you about the lamp
in his wrecked boat, deep in Tideglass Cavern. That's **"The Cavern Keeps a Light"**, and you'll
need Glimmerstep from the next region to reach it.

## Heading east

**Where next? East out of Pearlmoor on the fen road, to Lowleaf Hollow.**

Saltreach Fen lies east of town. **Don't go before you hold the Tide Gleam.** Its kin are around
level 16–18, and the fen-warden warns you as you step in. With Tidecall, the deep channels that
blocked the far side of the fen are now swimmable.

The **Pearlmoor spoke of the Lanternway** also wakes once you hold the Tide Gleam: from the fen,
a road runs back round the bay to the Vesper Crossroads (see the next chapter).
