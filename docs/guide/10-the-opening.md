## Tinderwick, before you have a lamp

**Where next? Out of the house, east along the Lanternway, to Fenn at the Vesper Crossroads
waystone.**

You wake in Tinderwick, a cosy coastal village at the blue hour. Gran is inside the cottage, and
the bed is a free rest whenever you want one.

When you first step outside, someone offers to explain how it all works. Say yes if you're new to
the genre: it's short.

Most players head for the north gate first. The **gate-warden** won't let anyone onto the coast
road without a kin, and tells you that **Star-tender Fenn** went east to the Crossroads waystone,
asking after you. Leave by the east side of town and follow the lamplit **Lanternway**. It's
safe: no wild kin will bother you before you have a partner.

At the waystone, Fenn is delighted to see you, then realises he left his **satchel** on the
Tinderwick store counter. Off you go again.

## The satchel and Duskapple Orchard

**Where next? West out of Tinderwick's square, through the gap in the hedge, to Duskapple
Orchard.**

The store keeper explains that a trade-cart courier swept the satchel up with her parcels and
went **west**. Walk to the square and out through the hedge gap into **Duskapple Orchard**, a
starlit old orchard one screen away.

- **Maudie** the courier is stuck with a three-wheeled cart. The satchel is on its tailboard.
  Take it.
- **Wren**, another young Wayfarer, is "minding the cart" (mostly by eating windfalls). Wren is
  your rival and your friend for the rest of the journey.
- **Grab the caches.** A windfall cache holds **2 Tallow Balms**, and a **Lumen Drop** hides in
  the orchard's south-west corner. A Lumen Drop raises a kin one level, so save it.

**Side trip: Wickstead Green.** The orchard's ride carries on west to Wickstead Green and its
**Lamp-school**, where a teacher explains each part of the game. It's optional and safe, and the
school's **Ottilie the Namewright** will rename kin for you later.

## Your vesperlamp and your first kin

**Where next? Take the satchel back to Fenn at the waystone.**

Out of the satchel come the **vesperlamp** and your first kin. Choose Vulpyre, Brinix or
Cloverkit (see [[catching-and-caring|Catching, Kindling & Caring for Kin]]). Fenn sends you home
with three jobs:

1. Catch a second kin in the **verge grass** by Tinderwick's north gate.
2. Collect the **Wayfarer's kit** the store keeper is holding for you.
3. Call on **Brisa Tallow** at Tinderwick's Lumenary.

On the walk home, a short lesson shows you how catching works.

## Catch a kin, then see Brisa

**Where next? Catch any wild kin in the verge grass, then visit Brisa at the Lumenary.**

The verge grass straddles the lane up to the north gate. Weaken a kin, then choose **LAMP**.

| Wild kin in and around Tinderwick | Type | Levels |
|-----------------------------------|------|--------|
| Wickmoth | Ember | 2–4 |
| Tallowpup | Ember | 2–4 |
| Ashlette | Ember | 2–4 |
| Wicklit | Light/Ember | 2–4 |
| Glimflit (orchard) | Light | 2–4 |

Brisa won't talk business until you've made a friend of your own. Once you have, she tells you
the truth: the Ember is relit from the **old Beacon** on the bluff, and its **wick-key** was lost
on the coast road. The old lamplighter who carried it still walks the Dimglass Coast.

## Dimglass Coast I

**Where next? North through the gate to the Dimglass Coast; find the old lamplighter near the
north end.**

The gate-warden lets you through now. The coast is a cliffside route running north, with a dry
lane all the way: the grass beside it is optional.

- **Wren** waits beside the lane for your first trainer battle: Glimflit (lv 5) and Brinelet
  (lv 6). It's friendly, and it teaches you how trainer battles go.
- **A star goes out.** Partway up the coast, a constellation vanishes from the sky. Nobody quite
  knows what it means yet.
- **The old lamplighter** stands near the north end, the only lit thing on the road. He gives you
  the **Beacon Wick-key**.
- **Caches** beside the lane hold a Tallow Balm, 2 Glow Charges and a small wick-purse.

| Wild kin on Dimglass Coast I | Type | Levels |
|------------------------------|------|--------|
| Brinelet | Tide | 3–5 |
| Lumpin | Tide/Light | 3–5 |
| Glimflit | Light | 3–5 |
| Pebbit | Stone | 3–5 (uncommon) |
| Brinix, Tidmote | Tide, Tide/Light | 4–6, in the offshore shallows once you can swim (Tidecall) |

**Catch a Pebbit or a Brinelet here.** Stone and Tide are both strong against Brisa's Ember team.

At the north end, **Brisa's coast-keeper** won't let you on to the flats until the Beacon burns.
That's your cue to go home.

**Teases to remember:** offshore lantern-buoys and a dark cave-mouth in the cliff. You can't
reach either yet. They open with Lantern Gifts you'll earn later.

## The Beacon

**Where next? Back to Tinderwick, and up the Beacon on the north-east bluff to the lantern
room.**

The wick-key opens the Beacon's foot door. It's three floors high, and Brisa's wick-tenders keep
the stairs. Both are sight trainers: they'll come to you.

| Floor | Trainer | Team | Purse |
|-------|---------|------|-------|
| I | Wick-tender Tansy | Wickmoth 6 | 120 |
| II | Wick-tender Cole | Tallowpup 6, Wickmoth 7 | 140 |

### Lampwarden Brisa Tallow (Ember)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Tallowpup | 6 | Ember |
| **Hearthkit** (ace) | 10 | Ember |

Purse: 600 wicks.

- **Bring:** Tide or Stone kin. A Brinix starter, or a Pebbit or Brinelet from the coast, does
  most of the work.
- **Avoid:** leaning on Verdant kin; Ember burns them.
- **Level:** about 8 is comfortable. If you're short, the verge grass and the coast make easy
  training. Rest in Gran's bed before you climb.

Win, and Brisa turns the wick-key. The great lantern blooms, the **Ember Gleam** answers in the
southern sky, and the **Lantern-fair** starts in the square below. Go down and enjoy it.

**Now in the shops:** Beacon Charges (×2.5 catch) appear once you hold the first Gleam.

## A letter for Fenn

After the star goes out on the coast, **Gran** has a letter for Fenn. This is the side quest
**"A Letter for Fenn"**: Fenn is watching the sky out on the Dimglass flats, which you reach in
the next chapter. Deliver it, then come home for her thanks (2 Tallow Balms and a Warm Balm).
