## Saltreach Fen

**Where next? East out of Pearlmoor and north through the fen to Lowleaf Hollow.**

The fen is a reed marsh crossed on raised plank causeways. The first half is walked; where the
planks give out, **Tidecall** lets you swim the deep channels into the second half, the Saltreach
Deeps.

Caches dot the reed isles: Glow Charges, Warm Balms and two purses on the first half, and a
Moth-amber on a far isle you swim to in the Deeps.

### Side quest: "The Quiet Reeds"

The **fen fisher** on the jetty in the Deeps asks you to re-kindle three lantern-reeds that have
gone dark, in channel order. The first two take. The third won't light, and nobody can say why.
Report back anyway for the **Marsh Lamp** (a ×2.0 catch charge in tall grass) and 200 wicks.

### Sunkbell Shallows

A half-flooded shrine off the Deeps, reached by swimming. It holds **2 Beacon Charges** and a
Warm Balm, and rare Tide kin: **Shimmral**, **Tidalarch** and **Tidmaren** (levels 17–20).

### The east-side ferry (for much later)

On the first half of the fen, an old ferryman called **Ansel** waits at a stage on the east
edge, and an eel-wife is worried about her boy. You can't cross yet; that story opens far later
in the north. See [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]].

### The road back to the Crossroads

A side road leaves the fen west along the bay: **Wrackline Path**, the **Sounding Cave** and
**Lanternfall Road**, ending at the Vesper Crossroads. It's the long way round, with a trainer or
two (Drifter Osk, Lamp-keeper Nell), and it's how the Pearlmoor side of the Lanternway connects
to the hub. You don't need it now. If you walk it, arriving at the Crossroads from a new side is
meant to feel like coming round, not getting lost.

## Lowleaf Hollow and the Glowmoss Bloom

**Where next? Talk to Sable Quill at the Lumenary, then help the town warm the Elder Bed.**

Lowleaf is a fern town glowing green from within, and its festival, the **Glowmoss Bloom**, is
in full swing. Two things stand out: the **Elder Bed** at the hollow's heart lies grey, and a
courteous, unsigned **letter** is pinned on the Lumenary notice-board. Read it.

- **The guest-bower** is the free rest.
- **The provisioner** sells Hearth Coals and Verdant Star-charts.
- **Wren** is in town, cheerful as ever.

**Sable Quill** is shy and would rather not make a speech. The Bloom won't crown over a grey bed:
warm the old moss first. This is the Gleam errand.

1. **The kilner** by the square needs dry **fen-wood** to fire the kiln.
2. The fen-wood is cached on the town's forest fringe, a lane held by two bloom-wardens.
3. Take it back. The kilner fires a **hearth-spore** (and pays you 150 wicks).
4. Tuck it under the Elder Bed. The bed blooms green and the festival gathers round.

### Lampwarden Sable Quill (Verdant)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Sporeling | 18 | Verdant |
| Dewling | 19 | Verdant/Tide |
| Glowsip | 20 | Verdant/Light |
| **Lumournis** (ace) | 22 | Verdant/Light |

Purse: 1,320 wicks.

- **Bring:** Ember (Vulcinder is ideal), Frost or Storm. Ember is resisted by Dewling's Tide half,
  so keep a second answer for that one.
- **Avoid:** Tide and Stone kin; Verdant drinks them up.
- **Level:** 19–20.

Win for the **Verdant Gleam** and the Lantern Gift **Glimmerstep**, which lets you walk into dark
caves and woods. Sable points you at the dark hollow-mouth on the town's north edge.

### Glimmerstep: what just opened

- **Glowmoss Deep**, the way on (below).
- **Spore Grotto**, off the lower floor of Glowmoss Deep.
- **Tideglass Cavern**, back on the Dimglass Coast. It's also where the old fisher's
  wreck-lamp is (see [[spurs-and-landmarks|Spurs, Landmarks & Side Quests]]).
- **The Lantern-car** at the Crossroads now runs its Lowleaf line, so Lowleaf is one ride from
  the hub.
- After the Gleam, a neighbour in the hollow mentions **Georgina**, who lives in the darkest dell
  past the ferns. That's the side quest **"The Sunniest House in the Dark"** (see
  [[spurs-and-landmarks|Spurs, Landmarks & Side Quests]]).

## Glowmoss Deep

**Where next? Through the dark of Glowmoss Deep to Cinderhead Mine.**

The deep wood is a glowing, branching interior across two floors, and your lamp is the only
moving light. Halfway through, you reach a clearing where the moss has gone **grey**: a gentle,
drained place, a sleeping kin, and a dark lantern. The people tending it are **the Hollowing**.
They are soft-spoken and sure they're helping: they only put the light to sleep, they say, so
nothing can be lost again.

Relight the lantern. The clearing blooms green again, the kin wakes, and a star comes back to the
sky. Wren arrives just after, unsettled, and wonders whether the Hollowing might have a point.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Moss-tender Dell | Sporeling 19, Sporemid 20 | 400 |
| Deepwood Warden Mirrel | Glowsip 20, Fennlight 21 | 420 |

Wild kin run 20–23: Mossglow, Fennlight, Sporeling, Sporemid.

Once the lantern is relit, the green chamber gets a bed of its own: Mossglow and Fennlight
(20–22), plus two kin you won't find wild anywhere else, **Mothdim** (18–19) and **Flutterwane**
(20–21), young moths drifting in to the relit light. Both are still below their kindling levels,
so this is the place to catch them in their first forms. The lower floor has a Star-chart
(Focus Mind), and the upper floor a Hearth Coal and a Warm Balm among its caches. A side cell
holds a Bright Balm, but your lamp needs to reach Brightlight (four Gleams) to show it.

### Side quest: "Spores for the Stall"

The Bloom's **stall-keeper** in Lowleaf has a bare shelf. Three spore-bundles lie along the glow
beds in Glowmoss Deep; a cross **Sporeling** (lv 21) has squatted on the last one and needs
shooing off. Bring all three home for a **Glow Salve** and 300 wicks.

## Cinderhead Mine and the Lamp-down vigil

**Where next? Talk to Otho Grist in the headframe hall, then go down Cinderhead Deep for the
vigil-lamp.**

Cinderhead is a miners' town at a mine mouth. The miners hold the **Lamp-down vigil**: lamps
lowered, waiting, because the old crew's vigil-lamp was left at the third gallery when the dark
came up. **Otho Grist** won't talk about a Gleam until you bring it up **still lit**.

- There's a rest in town, and the **pit-provisioner** sells balms and Hearth Coals.
- **Honest warning:** Otho is the first Lampwarden who can really stop an under-prepared party.
  The trip down for his lamp is your training.

### Cinderhead Deep (three floors)

The Deep is a ladder maze, gated by **Glimmerstep**. The vigil-lamp waits on the third, deepest
floor.

**Side quest: "The Foreman's Ledger."** A lone miner in town asks you to recover the old crew's
ledger from a side gallery in the Deep. Bring it back for a Starglass Shard and 300 wicks.

### Lampwarden Otho Grist (Stone)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Rubbol | 25 | Stone |
| Voltcrag | 26 | Stone/Storm |
| Riddlestone | 27 | Stone |
| **Ferrolith** (ace) | 28 | Stone |

Purse: 1,680 wicks.

- **Bring:** Tide, Verdant or Frost. **Frost hits Voltcrag four times over.**
- **Avoid:** Storm moves do **nothing** to Stone kin. Ember and Storm kin are both weak to Stone.
- **Level:** 26–27 after the Deep. Use a Lumen Drop if you're a level short.

Win for the **Stone Gleam**. With Verdant and Stone lit, the eastern quarter of the Crown is
whole. Otho gives no Lantern Gift; the Stone is a Gleam of endurance.

## Onward, and the shortcut home

**Where next? Back through the mine, out of the Deep's far door, and up Cinderwind Scarp.**

- **The far door.** The way north out of Cinderhead Deep was chained until Otho vouched for you.
  Now it opens onto **Cinderwind Scarp**.
- **The mine shortcut.** On the Deep's far side, open the sealed mine door from the inside. It
  links Cinderhead straight to the Vesper Crossroads for good.
- **The seam we don't work.** After the Stone Gleam, Otho waits by a sealed door in the mine's
  north workings. That's the way into the Old Seams (see [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]]),
  best left until you're stronger.
