Prices are the same at every counter in Vesperholm. Shops buy back any priced item at **half**
its price. Key items and quest charms can't be bought or sold.

## Medicine

| Item | Effect | Price |
|------|--------|------:|
| Tallow Balm | Restores 20 health | 120 |
| Warm Balm | Restores 60 health | 500 |
| Bright Balm | Restores all health | 1,200 |
| Hearth Coal | Rouses a **fainted** kin with half its health | 1,500 |
| Lumen Drop | Raises a kin one level | not sold |

Balms can't wake a fainted kin; only a Hearth Coal or a rest can. The seven troubles (status
conditions) are cured by resting. A few gifts heal too: the **Glow Salve** (60) from Lowleaf's
stall, and the **Warm Flask** (80) from the crag-tender on the Windward Stair.

## Other services

- **Hesper Vane**, who visits the inns, helps a kin remember a forgotten move: 250 wicks, charged
  only if it works.
- **Ottilie the Namewright** at the Wickstead Lamp-school renames your kin, free.
- **Rests** at inns, homes and firesides are always free.
