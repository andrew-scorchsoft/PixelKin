## Cinderwind Scarp

**Where next? Up the Scarp's ladders and north over the Ashfall Shelf to Galehigh.**

The Scarp is a cliff face climbed on the ore-haulers' ladders, which sit at opposite ends of
each band, so the way up zig-zags. The direct cut east to Galehigh is blocked by a **rockfall**;
a hauler beside it tells you to go "up and round by the Ashfall". Head north through the wood at
the top.

|---------|------|-------|
| Ore-hauler Hobb-ree | Oreling 28, Rubbol 30 | 480 |
| Kite-runner Rue | Squallox 29, Cirruff 29, Galefox 31 | 496 |

Wild kin run 28–30: Rubbol, Galefox, Thrumble, Lumecite, Nimbruff and Voltcrag.

## The Ashfall Shelf

The Ashfall is grey ash-grass on old slag tips, threaded by narrow lanes.

- **The haulers' camp** has a brazier where you can rest. It's your last rest before Galehigh.
- **Oda Kilnwright** sits at the camp. Her side quest, "The Last Pane", needs the Updraft Kite,
  so it's one for later in this chapter.
- **A slag nook:** a ledge drops into a walled pocket with a **Starglass Shard** under the ash.
  A second ledge drops you back out.
- **A walled west pocket**, reached round by the camp, holds a hauler's lunch-tin with a
  **Hearth Coal**.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Ore-hauler Sile | Sparkrat 30, Gravelo 32 | 512 |
| Gale-watch Pell | Flintbeak 31, Voltcrag 33 | 528 |

| Wild kin | Type | Levels |
|----------|------|--------|
| Gravelo | Stone | 30–33 |
| Sparkrat, Voltcrag | Stone/Storm | 30–33 |
| Flintbeak, Galefox, Thrumble | Storm | 30–33 |
| Pyrolith | Ember/Stone | 30–33 |
| **Stonebark** | Verdant/Stone | rare (its only wild home before the dawn) |

**Catch a Stonebark if you can.** Being part Stone, it takes nothing from Storm moves, which makes
it a great answer to Mira.

You'll also see an **old kiln stack** on the shelf's north lip and a **Windward lookout**. Both
need the Updraft Kite.

When you reach Galehigh's upper road, the haulers promise to clear the Scarp's rockfall, so the
short way back to the mine is open from then on.

## Galehigh Terraces and the Kite-rising

**Where next? Help the kite-maker rebuild her kite, fly it at the Kite-rising, and ride the winch
up to Mira.**

Galehigh is a stepped cliff-farm in the wind, all washing-lines and kites, and the **Kite-rising**
festival is under way. **Mira Vael** shouts down from far above: nobody meets the wind from the
ground. Raise a kite with the town and the winch will bring you up.

- **The Steady Gust Inn** is the rest. **The kite-stall** is the shop (Bright Balms appear once
  you hold the Storm Gleam).
- **The kite-maker's errand.** Her best kite blew apart in last night's squall. Find the
  **Kite Spar**, then the **Kite Sail**, then the **Kite Tail** on the lower terraces (each find
  points to the next). She rebuilds it, and you take it to the **winch-keeper** on the festival
  terrace.
- **Fly at the Rising.** The festival takes your kite up with the town's, and the winch hauls you
  to the **Skyloft**.

### Lampwarden Mira Vael (Storm)

Mira waits at the launch ledge at the head of the Skyloft.

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Thrumble | 30 | Storm |
| Glacewing | 31 | Storm/Frost |
| Flintbeak | 32 | Storm |
| **Strikeaven** (ace) | 34 | Storm |

Purse: 2,040 wicks.

- **Bring:** Stone kin. They take **no damage at all** from Storm moves and hit Storm hard. Frost
  is good too. Watch Glacewing: its Frost moves hurt Stone.
- **Avoid:** Tide and Verdant kin, which Storm hits hard.
- **Level:** 30–31.

Win for the **Storm Gleam** and the Lantern Gift **Updraft Kite**: ride warm thermals up terraces
and across short gaps.

### Updraft Kite: what just opened

- **The Wind-Eye**, a sky-grotto off Galehigh's high ledges: **Cumulance**, Hailwhirr and
  Glacewing, plus 2 Beacon Charges, a Starglass Shard and a Lumen Drop.
- **Windward Stair II** (the way on), and **Thunderroost** off it.
- **Kiln Gully**, through the Ashfall's kiln stack.
- **The Windward lookout** on the Ashfall, a kite crossing to Windward Stair I.
- **The Lantern-car's Galehigh line** at the Crossroads.

### Side quests at Galehigh

- **"The Last Pane"** (Oda Kilnwright, at the Ashfall camp). Ride the kiln stack's thermal into
  **Kiln Gully**, take the last pane of lamp-glass from the drying loft, and set it in the blind
  lamp by Galehigh's kite-stall. Reward: 1,200 wicks. Kiln Gully also holds a Beacon Charge and
  the **Star-chart: Shardstorm**, and its wild kin (33–35) include the rare **Scorchwing**.
- **"The Crag-tender's Kettle"** (the crag-tender on Windward Stair I). Pick the wind-burnt
  ledge-herb from a high Galehigh terrace (Updraft Kite) and carry it up to her. Reward: the
  **Warm Flask**, a strong heal.
- **"A Kite for the Waystone Kid"**: after the Storm Gleam, the kite-maker has a kite the
  Waykeeper ordered for the kid at the Vesper Crossroads. Ride the Lantern-car down and deliver
  it.

## The Windward Stair

**Where next? North up the Windward Stair to the Pale Vault Glacier.**

The lower switchbacks are walked; the wind-gap between the two halves needs the **Updraft
Kite**. Reaching the high crags opens a **drop shortcut** back down to Galehigh, a quick way home
for supplies.

| Trainer | Where | Team | Purse |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| Crag-hand Edda | Lower Stair | Sparkrat 34, Flintbeak 35 | 560 |
| Gale-watch Rowan | Lower Stair | Thrumble 34, Glacewing 35 | 560 |
| Crag-watch Merle | High crags | Flintbeak 35, Hailwhirr 36 | 576 |

Wild kin run 32–36: Flintbeak, Sparkrat, Thrumble, Glacewing, with Chillpip and Geolace higher up.

For colour, there are cairns to add a stone to on the way up, a climbers' shelter and a spyglass
at the top. **Thunderroost**, a dead-end off the high crags, holds the rare **Strikeaven** and the
**Star-chart: Tempest**.

## Pale Vault Glacier and the Lamp-Line

**Where next? Talk to Ysolde Frost by the undercroft door, then fetch aurora-oil for the
brackets.**

The glacier town is lonely and beautiful under the aurora, and its festival is the
**Aurora-watch**: the town stands on the ice in silence, each holding one lit lamp. **The Held
Flame Inn** is the rest. There's no shop here, so stock up at Galehigh first.

**Ysolde Frost** shows you seven dark lamp-brackets in the blue ice below her Lumenary. Light them
in order and let her see the light hold. They burn **aurora-oil**, and the tallow-keeper who
renders it has a doused hearth.

1. **Storm-kindling** is cached nearby in the approach hollows. Take it to the
   **tallow-keeper**; she renders the **aurora-oil** (and pays you 200 wicks).
2. **On the open ice, Warden Còr appears**, the leader of the Hollowing. He's courteous, sad and
   very persuasive. He doesn't fight; he asks whether the dawn is worth all the loss. Then he
   leaves you to think.
3. **Fenn** arrives just too late, and tells you that he and Còr were once fellow star-tenders.
4. **Wren** waits at the undercroft door, shaken by Còr's words. This is Wren's hardest fight so
   far: **Glimscout 39, Glacewing 39, Brinewrath 41** (984 wicks). Rest first.
5. **The undercroft.** The oil opens the door. Light the seven brackets **in order**, past two
   frost-wards.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Frost-ward Sela | Glaceling 37, Hushbore 38 | 760 |
| Frost-ward Orrin | Blizzrhare 38, Hushbore 39 | 780 |

Wild kin on the glacier run 36–40 (Glaceling, Blizzrhare, Iceling, Prismcub, Snowcune,
Glacewing, Prismantus); the undercroft adds Hushbore and Hushvole.

### Lampwarden Ysolde Frost (Frost)

Ysolde waits at the vault's heart, below the seventh bracket.

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Glaceling | 36 | Frost |
| Blizzrhare | 37 | Frost |
| Hushbore | 38 | Frost |
| Glacewing | 38 | Storm/Frost |
| **Prismantus** (ace) | 40 | Light/Frost |

Purse: 2,400 wicks.

- **Bring:** Ember first (Blazethorn, if your fox has grown), Solar if you have one, and Stone for
  Glacewing.
- **Avoid:** Verdant and Storm kin. Frost hits Stone hard too, so bring Stone out only against
  Glacewing.
- **Level:** 38–39.

Win for the **Frost Gleam** and the Lantern Gift **Emberward**, a tended ember that burns a path
through **coldfog**. The northern quarter of the Crown is whole.

### Emberward and other things that just opened

- **Hushfrost Pass**, west out of the glacier, is the way on. Its coldfog throat needs Emberward.
- **"The Aurora Sketcher"**: the painter at the Aurora-watch asks you to stand with her at three
  viewpoints (a Windward crag, the glacier shore and the festival ice). Reward: the **Aurora
  Charm**, ×2.5 on Frost kin.
- **"Wren's Ribbon"**: back at Galehigh, Mira has a kite-ribbon Wren dropped at the Rising. Leave
  it at the quiet ledge on the Windward Stair where Wren used to sit. It pays off later.
- **The Rimeway** climbs north out of Pale Vault, and **Isel** the lens-grinder by the north road
  has a request (see [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]]).
- **The Still Hour.** After the Frost Gleam, the aurora-watcher notices something odd in the deep
  ice (see [[the-three-hours|The Three Hours]]).
