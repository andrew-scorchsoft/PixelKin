The Old Light stands on Lightkeeper's Point, west of Pearlmoor Quay along the beach path. Its lamp
was taken out years ago, and in its place sits **Mr. Punchwheel**, a clockwork joke-engine with a
grinning brass horn. Every joke in the tower is the machine's own; a plaque in the lobby says so.

You climb it because Reyl Wash sends you, as part of "The Causeway Bell" (see
[[the-tide|Chapter 2]]). The door stays shut until Reyl has given you that errand.

## How the tower works

Each hall is entered in one corner, and the stairs up are in the other, so you always cross the
hall. A joke or riddle stands in front of each staircase. Choose the right line and the stair
opens. **Miss twice and Punchwheel blurts the answer and opens it anyway.** Nobody gets stuck
here.

**Moll**, Tam's old stage partner, waits one floor ahead of you all the way up, telling the
story a piece at a time. She doesn't battle. Listen to her: the tower is sadder and kinder than
it first looks.

Each floor also has an optional **alcove regular** you can battle for wicks and experience.

## Floor 1 — the Lobby of Groaners

The turnstile only turns for a laugh. Punchwheel tells you a lighthouse joke. **Laugh, groan or
explain it back: any answer works.**

- Regular: **the Usher** (The Smiler 10, Wickmoth 11), 176 wicks.
- Wild: Glimflit, The Smiler, Wickmoth, Riddlestone (9–11).

## Floor 2 — the Late Laugh

A little theatre where the audience laughs one line late. **The Heckler** stands between you and
the stairs.

- Keeper: **the Heckler** (JesterHalf 11, Glimflit 12), 240 wicks.
- **Answer: "Say nothing. Wait a beat."** Let the late laugh land on the punchline.
- Regular: **the Juggler** (JesterHalf 11, Pebbit 11, Glimflit 12), 192 wicks.
- Wild: The Smiler, JesterHalf, Glimflit, Spirlet, Lumpin (10–12).

## Floor 3 — Intermission

**A tea-urn here is a full rest**, and it becomes your wake-up spot. A big rubbery mouth wants to
know how a lamplighter gets over a broken heart.

- **Answer: "One rung at a time."**
- Regular: **the Pie-thrower** (Brinelet 12, McClownface 12), 192 wicks.
- Wild: **Snoozlet**, JesterHalf, **Spirlet** (10–12). Snoozlet and Spirlet are Lunar type,
  super-effective against Reyl's Tide team. Catch one here.

## Floor 4 — the Backwards Inn

The lobby again, but mirrored, with a small mirror maze. A backwards tea-urn rest stands beside
the Ringmaster.

- Keeper: **the Ringmaster** (JesterHalf 12, Riddlestone 13), 260 wicks. He poses the riddle.
- **Riddle:** "I go out twice a day and come back twice a day, but I never once leave the shore."
  Punchwheel is very confident it's a burglar. **Answer: "A tide."**
- Regular: **the Mirror** (Riddlestone 12, Riddlestone 13), 208 wicks.
- Wild: JesterHalf, Riddlestone, Spirlet, McClownface (11–13).

## Floor 5 — the Gallery of Confident Facts

Six captioned paintings. Only one caption is true. The hall's north lane is a strip of waxed
glare-ice (with banana skins). Punchwheel also has a knock-knock joke nobody has ever told; see it
through to the end.

- **Answer: "TAM AND REYL, THE FERRY."**
- Regular: **the Critic** (Spirlet 13, Snoozlet 13, Riddlestone 14), 224 wicks.
- Wild: McClownface, JesterHalf, Riddlestone, The Smiler (12–14).

## Floor 6 — the One Joke

Nothing to solve. The music drops away as you cross. Two cups by the rows of empty stools are a
rest.

- Wild: Spirlet, Snoozlet, McClownface (13–14).

## The Winding Room

**The green-room chair by the stairhead is a rest. Use it.** Then the machine's **headline act**
stands in the only gap to the drum where Tam's card has sat for eleven winters.

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| The Smiler | 14 | Light |
| McClownface | 14 | Tide/Light |
| **JesterHalf** (ace) | 15 | Light/Dark |

Purse: 300 wicks.

- **Bring:** Dark kin for the Light ones, or simply a well-rested level 13–14 party. Verdant or
  Storm handles McClownface.

Beat them and they step aside. Then comes the quiet part: Reyl's log, a cut rope, and **Tam's
card**, with its last line never finished.

- **Answer: "Tides go out to come back."**

You come away with the **netmender's floats**. A slide carries you back down to the Point. Take
the floats to the netmender on Pearlmoor Quay for the bell-rope.

## The tower's own kin

The Old Light's clowns live nowhere else in Vesperholm, and every one is cheerful rather than
creepy.

| Kin | Type | Kindles into |
|-----|------|--------------|
| **JesterHalf** | Light/Dark | JesterFull at lv 24 |
| **The Smiler** | Light | The Exciter (Light/Storm) at lv 22 |
| **McClownface** | Tide/Light | does not kindle |

**After the Tide Gleam** the lighthouse on the Point lights up again. Come back and look.
