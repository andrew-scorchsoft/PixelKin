> **Before you set out**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Recommended level** | 58 for the first Vigil, rising to 70 for the Last Lesson |
> | **Lantern Gifts needed** | All six |
> | **Where it starts** | A witness in Dawnstead, then Watcher Oriel at Nightreach |

With the sky relit, it starts to **shed**: star-shards shaken loose as the woken constellations
settle into their seats. Where each one lands, the strongest kin of that quarter gather, and one of
the **Vigilants** (the keepers who tended the lamps before the Long Dusk) comes out of retirement to
watch over it, and to test whoever comes to keep it.

The Vigilants are the game's first **full six-kin teams** with clever battle sense. Everything
about the chain is a celebration, not a grind: each site has a rich wild bed to train on, a rare
catch, and a one-off reward.

## How the chain works

1. **A witness in Dawnstead** has seen the first star fall. Not a constellation going out, but
   something shed, trailing gold.
2. **Watcher Oriel**, the junior watcher you met at Nightreach, now keeps the great telescope. She
   reads where the first shard fell and gives you a **reading**: a riddle-clue to a place.
3. **The reading opens the site**, a small sealed annex off a place you already know. Before you
   hold the reading, you can see the scar of starlight but can't enter.
4. **Keep the vigil.** Inside: apex-level wild kin, a rare register kin, and the **Vigilant's
   trial**. Win to take the **Starfall Shard** and the site's reward. The shard's glint reads the
   next fall, so each Vigilant gives you the next reading.
5. **Lost the thread?** Oriel repeats your current reading whenever you ask.
6. **Five shards** open the **Last Lesson** at the Umbral Spire's summit.

There's no rest inside a Vigil site, so rest nearby before you go in.

## The five Vigils

| # | Site | Where (the answer to the reading) | Wild levels | Register catch |
|---|------|-----------------------------------|-------------|----------------|
| I | **Hearthfall** | Tinderwick: on the bluff above the Beacon | 58–60 | Embralux, Tideveil (in the water) |
| II | **Grovefall** | Spore Grotto, off Glowmoss Deep: the far cell | 60–62 | Mycovast |
| III | **Stormfall** | Thunderroost, off the Windward Stair: the upper ledge | 62–64 | Nullhusk |
| IV | **Sunfall** | Sunvault Climb, the sun-vine bridges: behind the viewpoint | 64–66 | Helithorn, Dawnwatcher |
| V | **Murkfall** | Coldfog Marches, the deep marsh: the drowned jetty | 66–68 | Bogvast, Cindervast, Solarmourn |

### Vigil I — Hearthfall: Wick-Mother Esra

*"The first came down in the south, where the first lamp learned its name."*

| Kin | Level |
|-----|-------|
| Wicklord, Chandrek | 58 |
| Warmantis, Pyrolith, Scorchwing | 59 |
| **Embralux** (ace) | 60 |

Purse: 4,800 wicks. Reward: the **Radiant Charm** (×3.0 catch on a kin with any trouble). Her team
is all Ember, most with Light or Stone: bring **Tide** above all.

### Vigil II — Grovefall: Old Foreman Bramm

*"Under the hill, where the wood keeps its own weather and the moss has opinions."*

| Kin | Level |
|-----|-------|
| Lumenmoss, Rootwarden | 60 |
| Mycelarch, Lithonyx, Ferrolith | 61 |
| **Mycovast** (ace) | 62 |

Purse: 4,960 wicks. Reward: the **Star-chart: Tremor Quake**, the great Stone move the mining towns
never pressed. A Verdant and Stone team: bring **Frost**, which hits both (four times over on
Rootwarden and Mycovast).

### Vigil III — Stormfall: Ondra Vael

*"The roost where storms go when they're off duty."*

| Kin | Level |
|-----|-------|
| Tempestail, Glacitern | 62 |
| Frigalance, Cumulance, Vortexlope | 63 |
| **Nullhusk** (ace) | 64 |

Purse: 5,120 wicks. Reward: **the storm-tithe**, 5,000 wicks. Storm and Frost: bring **Stone**
(untouchable by Storm moves, though Frost hurts it) and Ember.

### Vigil IV — Sunfall: Dame Solenne

*"Where summer was put away for safekeeping."*

| Kin | Level |
|-----|-------|
| Sunstag, Lunaquell | 64 |
| Solreach, Lunvane, Solarmourn | 65 |
| **Helithorn** (ace) | 66 |

Purse: 5,280 wicks. Reward: the **Star-chart: Sunburst Nova**. A mix of Solar and Lunar kin, the
mirror axis: bring **Storm** for the Solar ones and **Dark** for the Lunar ones.

### Vigil V — Murkfall: Warden Mer

*"Where the water forgot how to speak."*

| Kin | Level |
|-----|-------|
| Liminalux, Voidmantle | 66 |
| Stillwarden, Wisprestored, Cindervast | 67 |
| **Bogvast** (ace) | 68 |

Purse: 5,440 wicks. Reward: the **Morrow Charm** (×3.0 catch on your first turn). Mostly Dark,
with Light kin mixed in: bring **Solar** above all, and keep Lunar moves at home.

## The Last Lesson

**Where: the summit of the Umbral Spire, once you hold all five shards.**

A gauntlet, back to back. Three Vigilants come for a rematch at full strength:

| Bout | Trainer | Ace |
|------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Ondra Vael | Nullhusk 69 |
| 2 | Dame Solenne | Helithorn 69 |
| 3 | Warden Mer | Bogvast 69 |
| — | *A heal* | — |
| 4 | **Star-tender Fenn**, at full strength | Dawnwatcher 70 |

Each Vigilant pays 5,520 wicks. If you lose a bout, the gauntlet starts again from the first one,
so go in rested and stocked with Bright Balms and Hearth Coals.

### Star-tender Fenn

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Glimscout | 68 | Light |
| Lunarbel | 68 | Lunar/Light |
| Crystalune | 68 | Lunar/Frost |
| Prismantus | 69 | Light/Frost |
| Helixia | 69 | Solar |
| **Dawnwatcher** (ace) | 70 | Lunar/Light |

Purse: 8,400 wicks, the biggest in the game. This is the hardest battle in Vesperholm, and it's
meant sincerely: your teacher, at his best. **Dark** kin cover most of his team (Lunar moves can't
touch them, and Dark hits Light and Lunar hard), with **Ember** for the Frost kin and **Storm** or
**Lunar** for Helixia.

### Dawnbrael

When Fenn falls, the five shards go into the dead ninth lantern, and **Dawnbrael** wakes: the
first-morning kin (Solar/Light, lv 70). It's a catch, and it never withdraws, so you can always
try again without re-fighting the gauntlet. Afterwards Fenn gives you his **Field-Glass**, and the
title that goes with it.

## After the Vigils

- Every Vigilant, and Fenn, can be battled again: the game's standing challenge circuit.
- **Helixia** starts blooming, very rarely, in Dawnstead's sunlit verge.
