Long before the Long Dusk, three watch-spirits turned Vesperholm's sky: one drew the evening in,
one kept the midnight watch, and one opened the morning. When the night fell and wouldn't lift,
the wheel jammed, and each Hour went to ground. They're the **Three Hours**, and each waits at
the end of a short quest, a puzzle, and the hardest wild battle you'll have met at that point.

All three are optional. Nothing in the main story needs them.

| Hour | Kin | Type | Where | Opens after | Level | Rests for |
|------|-----|------|-------|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Dusk | **Gloamber** | Ember/Dark | Tideglass Cavern | The Verdant Gleam (and Glimmerstep) | 38 | 10 won battles |
| Midnight | **Noctilune** | Lunar/Dark | The Hourfold, Pale Vault | The Frost Gleam (and Emberward) | 48 | 14 won battles |
| Dawn | **Erstmorn** | Solar/Light | The Unrisen Stair, Sunken Solarium | The Lunar Gleam (and Sunsketch) | 55 | 18 won battles |

## How the battles work

Each Hour is a **chance, not a gift**. It's far stronger than the kin around it, and if you knock
it out or run, it **withdraws** and rests until you've won that many battles elsewhere. Come back
and try again: the site tells you how many wins are left.

**Tips for all three:**

- Bring your best charges. A Beacon Charge, the Wrecklight Charm, the Bright Lamp or the Radiant
  Lamp all help a lot.
- Put it to sleep or chill it first if you can: a dozing or chilled kin is two and a half times
  easier to catch.
- Weaken it gently. A kin with a sliver of health left catches far more often than one at half.
- Don't spend the **Starlamp** here. You need it at the top of the Umbral Spire.

## The Dusk Hour: Gloamber

**Where to start: the netmender on Pearlmoor Quay, after you hold the Verdant Gleam.**

1. **The rumour.** Talk to the netmender after the Verdant Gleam. She's heard a low singing in the
   cliff at lamp-lighting time, coming up out of Tideglass Cavern.
2. **The wreck-lamp.** You need the old fisher's quest, "The Cavern Keeps a Light", underway
   (he's in Pearlmoor's inn after the moor-bell rings). Enter Tideglass Cavern with Glimmerstep and
   relight the stern-lamp of his wrecked boat at the far end.
3. **The verse.** With the lamp burning, read the etched glass hood: *"Last light first; the low
   light after; the deep light once the others hold."*
4. **The Lampwright's Relay.** Three standing glass lenses carry the wreck-lamp's beam deeper. Raise
   your lamp to them **in this order**:
   1. the **amber lens** on the west shelf,
   2. the **low lens** on the islet in the middle pool,
   3. the **deep lens** by the stair seam.

   Out of order, the glass stays cold. When the third takes, the floor rings like a bell and a
   glass stair opens.
5. **Tideglass Gallery.** Below, in the pooled light, the Dusk Hour lifts its head.

**Gloamber** is Ember/Dark: Tide and Stone hit its Ember half, and Light hits its Dark half. Tide
is the safest attacker at this point in the journey.

## The Midnight Hour: Noctilune

**Where to start: the aurora-watcher at Pale Vault, after you hold the Frost Gleam.**

1. **The rumour.** The aurora-watcher has noticed the sky bending round one fold of the deep ice,
   "like it's queuing".
2. **Ysolde's snuffer.** Ysolde Frost gives you the **Vigil Snuffer**. The Still Hour won't be seen
   by firelight: for once, you'll be putting lights out, on purpose.
3. **The Hourfold.** The way in is off the glacier's deep ice, through coldfog (Emberward). Inside,
   blue-ice terraces drop by one-way ledges. Two of the drops are wrong and send you back to the
   mouth; the true line runs **under the brightest aurora ribbons**. A Moth-amber waits in the
   pocket where the wrong drops land.
4. **The Unstruck Toll.** Three blue-white vigil-braziers burn on the bottom shelf. A sign reads
   the sky for you: *"the ribbon kneels east, then over the water-ice, then west."* Snuff them
   **east, then centre (out on the water-ice), then west**.
5. With the last flame out, a dome of midnight glass unrolls into a sentinel.

**Noctilune** is Lunar/Dark: Solar hits it four times over. Don't bring Lunar attacks (Dark
ignores them) or Light kin (Dark hits them hard).

## The Dawn Hour: Erstmorn

**Where to start: Nessa Cole at Nightreach, after you hold the Lunar Gleam.**

1. **The bell.** Nessa has heard, three nights running, a morning bell that doesn't exist, ringing
   due south, in the Solarium's drowned garden.
2. **Lucan's phial.** Lucan Pyre has kept one cupful of the last morning before the Long Dusk: the
   **First-Light Phial**.
3. **The basin.** At the Solarium's deepest fold, before a sealed processional stair, pour the
   phial into the dry sun-basin. All of it.
4. **The Bloom Ascent.** The Unrisen Stair climbs over still black water, crossable only by
   sun-vine (Sunsketch). Bloom the first vine from the basin's light, then find the **sun-mirror
   flower** on its landing and bend the daylight across the water to the far vine.
5. At the head terrace, something half-finished stands facing east. It has been facing east for
   years.

**Erstmorn** is Solar/Light: Storm and Lunar hit it hard (Dark only breaks even, because Solar
shrugs it off). It's the last and hardest of the three, placed on purpose just before the Spire.
