## Hushfrost Pass

**Where next? West from the Pale Vault through Hushfrost Pass; burn through the coldfog with
Emberward.**

The first half is a snowed canyon with a glare-ice pool to slide across. It ends in a throat of
**coldfog**, the Hollowing's creeping mist, which only **Emberward** pushes through. The second
half thins towards a faint golden glow ahead: the Solarium.

| Trainer | Where | Team | Purse |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| Coldfog Lampman Dunstan | First half | Hushbore 41, Crystarn 41 | 656 |
| Pass Survivor Hesper | First half | Vortexlope 42 | 672 |
| Thaw-tender Tilda | Second half | Geodrake 42, Prismantus 42 | 672 |

Wild kin run 40–42, mostly Frost: Hushbore, Crystarn, Geodrake, Blizzrhare, Prismcub, Vortexlope
and Glacitern. Near the fog you'll meet quieted kin too: **Stillwarden** (Frost/Dark) and
**Cindersob** (Dark/Ember). **Catch a Dark kin for Nessa** at the end of this chapter.

Caches in the first half hold 300 wicks, a Warm Balm and a Starglass Shard. A snow-hollow off the
lane hides **2 Bright Balms**, but only a Radiant lamp (all eight Gleams) shows it, so note it
for later.

### Side quests in the pass

- **"The Caretaker's Lamp."** In a shelter in the second half, an old caretaker sits with a kin
  the coldfog put to sleep. Her lamp needs **aurora-oil**, which you'll find in **Aurora Hollow**,
  an Emberward-gated spur off the pass. Reward: the **Bright Lamp**, a ×2.5 catch charge.
- **"The Pass-walker's Map."** Beside a cold crater in the snow, a pass-walker marks every fallen
  star she hears of. Stand by other falls (a scorch, a cinder) as you travel, then find her again
  at Nightreach. Reward: a Starglass Shard, and an unsettling pattern.

**Aurora Hollow** itself has rare Frost/Light **Frostholm**, plus 2 Beacon Charges and a
Starglass Shard.

## The Sunken Solarium and the Last-Warm-Day

**Where next? Talk to Lucan Pyre, then fetch three sunmote phials from the flooded halls.**

The Solarium is a half-drowned sun-garden that still remembers warmth. Its festival is the
**Last-Warm-Day**, and **Lucan Pyre** is its theatrical ringleader. His stage is dark and its
three braziers are dead. Fetch up the drowned daylight, spark by spark.

- **The rest** is in the Lumenary, the troupe's green-room.
- **The errand.** Three **sunmote phials** lie in the flooded inner halls. Swim there with
  **Tidecall** (yes, from all the way back in Pearlmoor). Each phial lights one brazier, in turn.
- **Sweep the water.** The flooded halls also hide a Hearth Coal, a Warm Balm, a Starglass Shard,
  a Moth-amber and a Beacon Charge. A dry causeway skips them, so don't.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Troupe Player Calla | Sunsprout 43, Helibud 44 | 704 |
| Troupe Player Orsino | Solvyne 44, Dawnfawn 45 | 720 |

The Solarium has the widest wild table in the game (42–46): Solar and Verdant/Solar kin in the
dry grass, and Lunar and Lunar/Tide kin in the water. Look out for **Gilpaw**, which kindles three
times, all the way to a legendary-tier form.

### Lampwarden Lucan Pyre (Solar)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Helibud | 42 | Solar |
| Solvyne | 43 | Verdant/Solar |
| Goldmane | 44 | Solar/Stone |
| Sunstag | 44 | Solar/Verdant |
| **Solreach** (ace) | 46 | Solar |

Purse: 2,760 wicks.

- **Bring:** Storm and Lunar. Storm hits Solvyne and Sunstag four times over, but does nothing to
  Goldmane (it's part Stone): use Tide or Lunar on that one.
- **Careful:** Solar hits Lunar just as hard as Lunar hits Solar, and it hits Frost too.
- **Level:** 44–45.

Win for the **Solar Gleam** and the Lantern Gift **Sunsketch**: release a pocket of daylight and
shut night-flowers bloom into bridges.

### After the Gleam

- **"The Troupe's Sun-mask."** A troupe player has lost the gilt sun-mask in a side room off the
  flooded halls. Swim in and bring it back for the **Sun Charm** (×2.5 on Solar kin) and 300
  wicks.
- **Helia Vault**, off the Sunvault Climb, is a Sunsketch puzzle (below).
- **The Wick-Run**, a downhill run off the Sunvault Climb's foot, is open to anyone with a few
  wicks for a rented runner (see [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]]).

## The Sunvault Climb

**Where next? Up the Sunvault Climb's four stages to Nightreach Observatory.**

The climb is a gilt mountain face: ladders, sun-vine bridges, a trestle you cross over and then
pass under, and rope-planks on the Sun Ledges. The first bridge is dead until **Sunsketch**
blooms it.

| Trainer | Stage | Team | Purse |
|---------|-------|------|-------|
| Terrace-tender Bel | Foot | Solvyne 46, Sunstag 47 | 752 |
| Sky-watcher Tam | Sun-vine bridges | Lunveil 47, Helicore 48 | 768 |
| Star-watcher Orrin | Gilt Face | Lunveil 48, Drowshorn 48, Lunvane 49 | 784 |
| Sun-keeper Imma | Gilt Face | Solray 48, Helicore 48, Solreach 49 | 784 |
| Star-watcher Pim | Sun Ledges | Petalune 48, Goldmane 49 | 784 |

- **The keepers' shelter** on the Gilt Face is a rest.
- **The Watcher's Striker** is in a cache on the sun-vine bridges stage. **Pick it up**: Nessa's
  errand needs it.
- Other caches: a Bright Balm, purses, a Moth-amber, Beacon Charges, a **Lumen Drop** on the Gilt
  Face, and 2 Bright Balms on the Sun Ledges.

**Helia Vault**, off the sun-vine bridges, is a sealed reliquary crossed by blooming vines in the
right order and bouncing daylight off a sun-mirror flower. Inside: the rare **Heliovast**, the
**Star-chart: Sunburst Nova** and 2 Beacon Charges.

## Nightreach Observatory and the Vigil of the Seven

**Where next? Talk to Nessa Cole at the eyepiece, then light the seven watch-lamps on the Astral
Walk.**

Nightreach is a domed star-temple under the densest stars in the game, keeping the **Star-vigil**.
**The Long Watch Inn** is the rest. **Nessa Cole**, the most haunted of the Lampwardens, asks you
to light seven watch-lamps along the telescope terrace, one for each constellation you've given
back, in the order they came home.

- **Lamp 1** wants the old watcher's striker. You picked it up on the climb (if not, it's on the
  sun-vine bridges stage).
- **Lamp 5**: Fenn waits here and lights it with you. You can't out-fight Còr, he says; you'll
  have to out-remember him.
- **Lamp 6**: Wren is sitting under it, settled at last, and asks for a friendly bout: **Glimscout
  49, Frigalance 49, Brinewrath 50** (1,200 wicks). If you left Wren's ribbon on the Windward
  Stair, Wren mentions it.
- **Lamp 7**: Nessa names what Còr is building: the **Great Null**, a lantern made to hold no
  light, aimed at the **Keystar**, the one star all the others rekindle from.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Junior Watcher Lira | Drowshorn 49, Lunvane 50 | 1,000 |
| Junior Watcher Os | Nightwraith 50, Crystalune 51 | 1,020 |

Wild kin around the observatory run 48–52: Drowshorn, Nightwraith, Snoozlet, Spirlet, Lunveil,
Petalune, Lunvane and the rare **Dawnwatcher**.

### Lampwarden Nessa Cole (Lunar)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Drowshorn | 48 | Lunar |
| Nightwraith | 49 | Lunar |
| Crystalune | 50 | Lunar/Frost |
| Lunvane | 50 | Light/Lunar |
| **Lunarbel** (ace) | 52 | Lunar/Light |

Purse: 3,120 wicks.

- **Bring:** Dark kin. **Lunar moves do nothing to Dark kin**, and Dark hits Lunvane and Lunarbel

  answer (it hits Crystalune four times over).
- **Avoid:** Tide kin, which Lunar hits hard.
- **Level:** 50–51.

Win for the **Lunar Gleam** and the last Lantern Gift, **Starreach**: step across short voids of
pure dark.

## The Crown is whole

All eight Gleams are lit. The **Skyweave Crown** is whole, the **Penumbra** parts, and the road
to the Umbral Spire opens.

- **TRAVEL** appears in the pause menu: fast travel between every town you've visited.
- **The Lantern-car's Nightreach line** wakes, so all three cars now run from the Crossroads.
- **"Charting the Dark"**: the junior watcher asks for star-readings from three high points (a
  Sunvault terrace, the observatory roof, and, if you're brave, the Coldfog edge). The first two
  are enough. Reward: 400 wicks and a chart that names **Starwell**.
- **The Lost Hour.** Nessa hears a bell that hasn't rung (see
  [[the-three-hours|The Three Hours]]).

## The Coldfog Marches (optional)

Behind Nightreach, a back door leads down into the **Coldfog Marches**, through coldfog you burn
with Emberward. It's the one place in Vesperholm that stays dark however many Gleams you hold:
what the whole land would become if the Hollowing had their way.

- Hollowing acolytes keep the roads, and there's no inn in the fog. Wild kin run 46–50 and are
  mostly Dark: Voidmantle, Liminalux, Embergone and the grey dream-moth Lullwing.
- **North through the fog** lies **Stillwater**, the Hollowing's own village, with a rest, a shop
  and three side quests, and beyond it the Mourning Fields.
- **Drownlight Beacon**, a snuffed lighthouse off the first marsh, is now a tower you can climb
  (Emberward), with a shutter puzzle and its keeper at the top.
- All of this has its own chapter: [[the-hollowings-country|The Hollowing's Country]].
- **Hollowfen Stillworks**, a derelict null-works off the deep marsh, opened with **Glimmerstep**.
  It's quiet, sad and enormous. The charged-husk kin **Whorlix** lives here.
- **Valuables:** an Embergloss and a Murk Pearl lie in the marsh, worth 600 and 1,500 wicks.
