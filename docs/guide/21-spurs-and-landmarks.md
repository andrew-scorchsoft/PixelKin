Vesperholm is built so that a Lantern Gift earned in one valley reopens places you walked past
in another. Keep a mental list of the buoys you couldn't reach and the cave-mouths you couldn't
enter. This chapter is that list.

## Wickstead Green and the Lamp-school

West of Duskapple Orchard, through a cart-gate, lies a village green with a schoolhouse. It's
safe ground, open from the very first minute. Each teacher explains one part of the game, as
often as you like:

| Teacher | Lesson |
|---------|--------|
| Mistress Pennyweather (at the board) | Types and matchups |
| Master Tolly | Catching |
| Old Quennell | The seven troubles (status conditions) |
| Tutor Fernly | Kindling, new moves and bond |
| The Hearthkeeper | The Hearth |
| Master Mallow (the sick-bay cot) | Resting, rest points, fainting and Cannot die. The cot is a real rest. |
| Bunce (the tuck-shop stall) | Wicks, shops and Star-charts |
| Wayfarer Esk (by the pond) | Lantern Gifts and Lamplight |
| Odo, a pupil | The menus |

**Ottilie the Namewright** keeps the school's big book of names and will rename any of your kin.
A lost-property cache under the bluff holds a Tallow Balm.

## Gullcry Rock

Out along the buoy line from the Dimglass flats. Swim there as soon as you have **Tidecall**.

- **Glostern**, the rare harbour-light kin, swims in the surf.
- The **Tide Charm** (×2.0 catch) waits at the high stone.
- One of the five **driftletters** washes up here (see [[farlamp-isles|The Farlamp Isles]]).

## Tideglass Cavern and "The Cavern Keeps a Light"

A sea-cave of smoothed glass in the Dimglass cliffs, opened by **Glimmerstep**.

After you ring the moor-bell in Pearlmoor, the **old fisher** in the quayside inn tells you about
his wrecked boat. Its lamp still sits deep in Tideglass. Find the wreck at the cavern's far end,
relight its stern-lamp, then tell him it still burns. Reward: the **Wrecklight Charm** (×2.5
catch).

- **Caches:** 2 Warm Balms on the west shelf and 250 wicks in the wreck's strongbox.
- **A Starlight nook.** Once your lamp reaches **Starlight** (six Gleams), a deeper fold reveals a
  Starglass Shard.
- **Wild kin:** Brinelet, Shimmral, Brineroll, Fossik, Glostern, and the rare **Glostrael**.
- **The Dusk Hour** sleeps below. See [[the-three-hours|The Three Hours]].

## Spore Grotto

Off the lower floor of Glowmoss Deep. The grotto's rare find is **Mycelarch** (Verdant/Dark),
alongside Sporemid, Sporeling, Fennlight and Glowsip. A Starglass Shard and a **Lumen Drop** are
hidden inside.

## "The Sunniest House in the Dark" (Georgina)

After the Verdant Gleam, a neighbour in Lowleaf Hollow tells you about **Georgina**, who lives in
the darkest dell past the ferns with a hundred cats and isn't a bit afraid of the dusk. The path
north-east out of the hollow opens to **Gloamwood Dell**.

1. **Meet Georgina** in her cottage: candles, cats and a lot of fairy-lights. One of her kittens,
   **Pim**, bolted into the dell when the Hollowing's quiet swept through.
2. **Find Pim** in the darkest corner of the dell.
3. **Bring her home.** Georgina asks for a battle.

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Hearthkit | 24 | Ember |
| Warmantis | 25 | Ember/Light |
| **Gloampurr** (ace) | 27 | Lunar/Solar |

Purse: 648 wicks.

Afterwards she introduces the pride of the house, a **Gloampurr**: a dusk-coloured dragon-cat that
carries its own little sunrise. This clutch had a kitten spare, and she gives it to you (lv 5).
Raise her with sunshine.

## The Wind-Eye and Thunderroost

Two Updraft Kite spurs in the North. The **Wind-Eye** is a sky-grotto off Galehigh's highest
ledges with the unique Storm kin **Cumulance** and a good haul: 2 Beacon Charges, a Starglass
Shard and a Lumen Drop. **Thunderroost** is a dead-end ledge off the Windward Stair's high crags,
home to the rare **Strikeaven** and the **Star-chart: Tempest**.

## Helia Vault

A sealed reliquary off the Sunvault Climb's sun-vine bridges. It's a Sunsketch puzzle: bloom one
vine to reach a sunnier ledge, bloom the next from there, and bounce a pocket of daylight off a
sun-mirror flower to reach a vine you can't get at directly. Inside: **Heliovast** and the
**Star-chart: Sunburst Nova**.

## Lumen Drops: where to find them

A Lumen Drop raises a kin one whole level. You can't buy them anywhere.

| Where | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Duskapple Orchard | South-west corner |
| Spore Grotto | Off Glowmoss Deep |
| The Wind-Eye | Updraft Kite |
| The Gilt Face (Sunvault Climb) | On the main path |
| The Wick-Run | A ledge you can only reach by riding a ramp |
| The Old Seams | A ledge off the scree, riders only |
| The Bell Cellar, under Lowtinder | Beside the old town bell |
| Gran Tallow's reef battle | Three, after the dawn |
| The Farlamp Isles and the Undertide | Several, after the dawn |
| Deep-water caches | Off Gullcry Rock and the Dimglass flats, reachable only by skiff after the dawn |
