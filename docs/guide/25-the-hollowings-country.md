All through the journey you meet the Hollowing's *works*: drained lamps, quiet camps, null-works.
Up here, north of the Coldfog Marches, you finally meet the Hollowing as people. They're tired,
grieving and kind, and quite sure the dark is a mercy. Nobody up here will attack you. "You're the
one relighting the sky," they say, and ask you to keep your voice down because the kin are
sleeping.

Everything in this chapter is optional. None of it grants a Gleam or a Gift, and none of it leads
into the Umbral Spire. It's the late game's best detour all the same: a village, three quests, a
lighthouse puzzle, a new family of kin, and the strongest wild beds before the summit.

**Bring Solar kin.** Nearly everything here is Dark, and Solar hits Dark hard (and Frost/Dark and
Lunar/Dark kin four times over). Light kin hit Dark too. Leave your Lunar moves at home: they do
nothing at all to Dark kin.

## Getting there

**Where next? Nightreach Observatory's back door, with Emberward.**

There are two ways into the north:

1. **Nightreach's back door.** Behind Nightreach Observatory, the fog road leads down into the
   deep marsh, the **second Coldfog Marsh**. Its coldfog needs **Emberward**, and so does the
   crossing north into the near marsh.
2. **The Rimeway.** Once you've lifted the **Rime Gate** from the marsh side (see
   [[the-wild-ways|The Wild Ways]]), you can walk up from Pale Vault Glacier and down the
   Rimeway's far side. The descent's west road runs into the deep marsh, and its north road
   climbs the col into the Mourning Fields.

The north is a **loop**: the near marsh, Stillwater, the Mourning Fields, and back to the near
marsh. The Mourning Fields' south road drops down to the Rimeway. Coming round to somewhere you've
been isn't a wrong turn; it's the way the roads were built.

**The golden patch.** A survey peg stands by the gold grass on the near marsh's east edge. Read
it: the Solarium's light once came this far down the old water, and some of it soaked in. That's
why a rare **Auravane** (Verdant/Solar) roots here some nights, the only one in the fog.

Looking for a young **Mothdim** or **Flutterwane**? They don't live up here. Their only
wild home is the grey chamber in Glowmoss Deep, once you've relit its lantern (see
[[the-east|Chapter 3]]).

The older finds are still here: the **Drownlight Beacon** off the near marsh's west edge, the
**Hollowfen Stillworks** (Glimmerstep) in the deep marsh, an **Embergloss** and a **Murk Pearl**.

## Stillwater

**Where next? The inn for a rest, then talk to everyone. Three of them have something to ask.**

The **Still Water** is a mere under the north crags. It went still the winter the dark came and
has never rippled since. Round it sit shuttered lamp-posts, a meeting-house, an inn, a lamp-maker's
workshop and a handful of homes. Kin sleep under grey blankets by the doors. Hearths burn with their
faces to the wall behind latched louvred screens: still warm, just not looked at.

| Place | What's there |
|-------|--------------|
| **The Shutter** (the inn, west of the square) | A free rest. It's a rest point. |
| **The Quiet Hall** (the square) | The meeting-house and the **shutter-wall**, where the names of every lamp they put out are painted. **Keeper Wenna** runs it. At the back, by the benches, an acolyte keeps the **ledger of put-out lamps** on a reading lectern. |
| **The Dark-Lamp Maker** (east) | **Osric**, a lamplighter who now makes hoods and shutters. His counter sells Warm Balm, Bright Balm, Hearth Coal, Glow Charge, Beacon Charge and the **Hooded Lamp** (600 wicks: hood it to halve wild encounters). |
| **Margery and Edwy's Cottage** (south-west) | An old couple who came up from the coast so they wouldn't watch their kin grow old. Their hearth is screened and their stove is banked behind an iron door until the dawn. |
| **Maren's House** (south-east) | Linnet's mother. Part of "A Light Left On". |

Worth a word: **Elder Ashe** by the square, **Grete** who folds the sleepers' blankets, **Lisbet**
at the end of the jetty, watching the water, **Corwen** the doubter (who dreams in orange),
**Nell** on the shore walk, **Odo** sweeping the lanes, **Hallam** at the well, and **Rosalind**
among the names in the hall.

**Two short cuts.** Not every lane here was ruled by a surveyor. The **Willow Path** is a worn
diagonal from the west end of the shore walk up to the old willow by the water. The **Back Walk**
bows up between the two homes in the south and joins their door lanes: it's the villagers' own
way between the Coldfog gate (south-west) and the Mourning Fields gate (south-east), and it keeps
off the grey meadow, so nothing wild jumps you on it.

**A waystone.** Stillwater has a waystone by the Quiet Hall. Once you've been here, it's on the
**TRAVEL** list, so the long fog road only has to be walked once.

**Two caches.** A basket in the north-west meadow (**2 Bright Balms**), and a fisher's tin on the
far shore under the First Lamp (**2,000 wicks**).

### "A Light Left On"

**Linnet**, a small girl near the square, has found her gran's candle and lit it, which nobody in
Stillwater is meant to do. She gives you **Linnet's Candle** in its jar and lets you choose:

- **Hide it** in the hollow of the **old willow** by the water (north-west of the square). It burns
  there where only she can see it.
- **Show it** to her mother, **Maren**, in the house with the dormer. Maren remembers she used to
  keep a candle in every window.

Both endings are kind. Go back to Linnet either way and she gives you a **Starglass Shard**.

### "The Way Home"

**Tessaly** used to keep a lamp-shop on Pale Vault's glacier road. She wants to go home, but every
time she reaches the edge of the village her feet stop. Agree to walk ahead with your lamp, then:

1. Find her in the **near marsh**, by the table someone laid for three and never came back to.
2. Find her in the **deep marsh**, where the fog is worst.
3. Meet her **at home in Pale Vault**, on the west side of the glacier town.

Reward: **Tessaly's Lamp**, the lamp she hung in her shop window so the lost could find the door.
It's a keepsake charge: it burns at **×2.0 on a kin below a quarter of its health** (an ordinary
lamp on a healthier one), and it's **never spent**, so you keep it after every throw. Wear a kin
down into the red, then raise it.

### "The Shutter-wall"

**Where next? Read the wall in the Quiet Hall, then talk to Keeper Wenna.**

Wenna asks you to find three of the lamps whose names are on the wall, still standing where they
were put out, and read the note left at each:

| Lamp | Where |
|------|-------|
| The Wayfare Lamp | The near marsh, on the east side south of the long crossing |
| The Market Lamp | The deep marsh, south, near where a star fell |
| The First Lamp of Stillwater | Stillwater's far shore, north-east, round the end of the water. Read its note from the shore below it |

You can find them before you talk to her, but their brass plates are too tarnished to read until
you know whose they are. Each time you check in, Wenna names the lamps you still haven't found. Bring all three back to Wenna for a **Lumen Drop** and **1,500 wicks**,
and the reasons, in their own words, why the Hollowing did it.

## The Mourning Fields

**Where next? Out of Stillwater's south-east road, or east from the near marsh.**

These were Stillwater's farms. When the light went and the barley wouldn't ripen, the field-kin
kept waking up hungry in the dark. So the Hollowing let them sleep where they lay, tucked under grey
blankets woven from the coldfog. The scarecrows are lamp-posts with their lanterns hooded in wool.
Nothing here is hurt; it's all just asleep. Your first steps here add the Fields to your
Wayfarer's Charts and their entry to the LORE codex.

**The bank.** A long scree ledge runs across the west half of the fields. You can hop **down** it
anywhere, which makes it the quick way from Stillwater to the near marsh's gate. You can't climb it.
The one way **up** is a **stone stair** in the bank, east of the middle, with a hooded lamp beside
it. From the marsh gate, walk east across the barley drifts to the stair, climb it, then go back
west along the top to Stillwater. The **wool-gatherer** picking fog-wool off the fences by the
gate explains it too.

**The south crossing.** Below the sleepers' field, a broad drift of grey long grass runs right
across the fields, its edges lobed and wandering. The road to and from the Rimeway col has to
go through it, so expect a wild kin or two whichever way you're heading.

- **The field-tender** watches the sleepers' field in the middle. She tucks them in every evening.
- **The field shrine** sits on a shelf to the east. You'll see it long before you reach it: the only
  way onto the shelf is a drop from the upper terrace, and the way off is a ledge. On the little
  shelf behind it, where harvest-sweets were left for the field, is a **Lumen Drop**.
- **The east terrace.** On the last post of the top field, east of the stair's head, hangs the
  **Harvest Knot**, the year's last sheaf plaited and ribboned. It's a **key item**: hang it on
  your lamp from ITEMS (or pack it away again) and, while it's hung, wild kin come at you only a
  quarter as often. It does the Hooded Lamp's job better, and the two don't add together. It can't
  be sold. Show it to the field-tender and she'll tell you to keep it.
- **The west plot.** In its corner, under a fold of sacking, are **2 Lullwool Charms**: charges
  that burn at **×3.0 on a dozing kin** (and are ordinary lamps on a waking one). They're never
  sold.

### The Brook Piece

The shrine board names five fields, and one of them, **the Brook Piece**, has no road to it. It's
the little bramble-hedged nook in the **west corner of the col**, down by the Rimeway road. You can
see it over the hedge from the road, but the hedge won't let you in. The way in is round the back:
walk through the **long grass along the west wall**, then slip **under the snow pine's skirts**
into the nook.

Inside are a **marker stone** for the field, a tin under the hedge with a **Hearth Coal** in it,
and **Tussock**, the field-tender's own old Hushvole, asleep alone under the best blanket in the
Fields. Let him sleep: he was the first she tucked in, and the hardest. After the dawn he's up
and nosing through the stubble, his blanket folded (badly) beside him.

**Lumournis.** Read the marker stone all the way down. Someone has scratched a note asking you to
let the Brook Piece's long grass be: a big green moth with leaf wings comes down to it some nights
to sit up with Tussock, and it never goes to any other field. That's **Lumournis** (Verdant/Light),
and this patch of long grass is its **only wild home** anywhere in Vesperholm. Before the dawn
it's rare (51–52); after the dawn it's an uncommon find (59–62), and if you've met Tussock the
field-tender will tell you it's still there. Lullwing, Mycelarch, Crystalune and a Lunar/Dark kin share the grass with it.

## The Drownlight

**Where next? The lighthouse off the near marsh's west edge (Emberward). Climb to the lantern.**

The Drownlight was the north coast's light, and the first lamp the Hollowing ever put out. Its
keeper banked it low and bolted iron shutters round it fourteen winters ago, three winters before
the sky went dark. A keeper never kills a lamp, though. It's still burning up there behind the
iron, and its light leaks down the tower's old **light-wells**.

After so long in the dark, that leaked light is a **glare** nobody can walk through. Step into it
and you throw up an arm and stagger back.

**How the shutters work.** Each floor has **shutter-cranks** on the walls. A crank swings one
louvre: the glare leaves one doorway and pours across another. A little pennant on the crank shows
**WHITE** (as you found it) or **RED** (thrown). You can always wind a crank back, and every floor
is built so you can never get stuck for good. An enamel notice beside the first crank you meet
(on Floor I's hall wall) spells all this out. An acolyte's advice: *think about where the light
goes, not where it is.*

| The foot (the ruined quay) | Voidmantle, Shutterwick, Lullwing, Liminalux | 48–50 |
| I, the Oil Stores | **Shutterwick** (common), Voidmantle, Lullwing, Stillwarden, Liminalux | 50–52 |
| II, the Lens Gallery | **Shutterwick** (common), Voidmantle, Lullwing, Prismantus, Stillwarden; rarely Liminalux | 51–53 |

At the foot, the keepers' cache on the islet stone holds a **Bright Balm** and a **Beacon Charge**,

### Floor I: the Oil Stores

The floor is four rooms. You come in at the **hall** (south). Above it are the **west store** (with
Odell, and the **store crank** on its back wall) and the **stair room** (north-east, with the stair
up). A small **nook** opens off the hall's west end. The hall connects to each store by a doorway;
the two upper rooms connect to each other by a doorway in the middle.

The hall is the keepers' old living floor, and it's worth a slow walk: the mess table, the log
lectern, oil barrels with a tally of winters chalked on them, sandbags kept dry for tides that no
longer come, and crates of spare chimney-glasses that were never opened. Most of them have
something to read.

When you arrive, the glare blocks the hall's **east** doorway (to the stair room) and the doorway
**between** the two upper rooms.

**Hint.** You only need one crank on this floor, and it isn't the one in the store. Visit the nook
first.

**Full solution:**

1. Walk west into the **nook** and take the **Starglass Shard**. (The nook is only open before you
   throw the hall crank.)
2. Throw the **hall crank** on the hall's north wall, between the two doorways. The glare leaves
   the door between the upper rooms and fills the nook's doorway instead.
3. Go up the west doorway into the **west store** (Odell is waiting).
4. Walk east through the now-dark middle doorway into the **stair room**, and take the stair up.

Leave the **store crank** alone. Throw it and the glare jumps onto the west doorway you came in
by. That never helps, and if the hall crank isn't thrown yet it shuts you in the store until you
wind the store crank back.

**The stair needs Emberward:** coldfog has pooled in the stairwell, and only a tended ember pushes
through.

### Floor II: the Lens Gallery

The gallery is six rooms, each a different shape, set in a ring round the middle of the tower:

- **The watch room** (north-east, an L), where the stair from Floor I comes up. It holds the
  keepers' **watch-cot**, the old logs on a lectern and the **arrival crank**. Its leg runs south to
  a doorway down into the east gallery.
- **The round lens room** (north, in the middle), with the spare lens on its cradle and the
  grinding bench. Doorways lead east to the watch room and west to the stair turret.
- **The stair turret** (north-west), with the old turning-gear and the stair up to the lantern.
  A doorway in its south side leads down into the west gallery.
- **The east gallery** (south-east), a tall, narrow hall with Prue and the **east gallery crank**.
- **The south gallery**, a long watch passage with the old foghorn, a bench and Hask. The
  **south gallery crank** sits in a small bay off its north side.
- **The west gallery** (south-west, an L with a long arm north), with an oil cistern at its foot.

**Rest first.** The watch-cot is a free rest and a rest point.

When you arrive, the glare blocks **both** doorways along the top (watch room to lens room, and
lens room to stair turret), and the doorway between the east and south galleries. The direct way
west is barred twice over.

**Hint.** You can't go straight across the top. Go down and round the south side, open the far
doorway from below, then come back and open the near one last.

**Full solution:**

1. From the watch room, go **south** down the L's leg into the **east gallery** (Prue).
2. Throw the **east gallery crank**. The glare leaves the doorway into the south gallery (and moves
   to the doorway between the stair turret and the west gallery, which you won't need).
3. Go west into the **south gallery** (Hask, by the foghorn).
4. *Optional, but do it now:* go on west into the **west gallery** and look behind the **oil
   cistern** for a **Lumen Drop**, then come back.
5. Throw the **south gallery crank** in its bay. The glare leaves the doorway between the lens
   room and the stair turret (and now blocks the doorway into the west gallery, which is why step 4
   comes first).
6. Go back east into the **east gallery**, and north up the leg into the **watch room**.
7. Throw the **arrival crank**. The glare leaves the doorway into the lens room (and moves to the
   doorway down to the east gallery).
8. Walk west through the **round lens room** into the **stair turret**, and up to the lantern.

### The Lantern Room: the Shutterkeeper

**Maudry Kell**, the **Shutterkeeper**, kept the Drownlight thirty-one winters, and then put it out
herself. She greets you from the stairhead and asks you over to the lamp. She won't light it for
you, but she'll battle you for an answer. (The great lamp itself is chained to its post until
you've heard her out; even after the dawn, it tells you to ask her first.)

| Kin | Level | Type |
|-----|-------|------|
| Stillwarden | 52 | Frost/Dark |
| Liminalux | 53 | Dark/Light |
| (a Lunar/Dark kin) | 53 | Lunar/Dark |
| **Shutterwick** (ace) | 54 | Dark/Light |

Purse: **3,240 wicks** (a Lampwarden's rate). It's a one-time bout.

- **Bring:** Solar above all (four times over on Stillwarden and the Lunar/Dark kin, twice on the
  rest). Ember and Stone also hit Stillwarden; Light hits every Dark half. **Avoid** Lunar moves,
  and be wary of the doze: she plays to sleep.
- **Level:** about 52–54. The watch-cot is one floor down.

Afterwards she tells you why: a daughter rowing home towards this light in the fog, and thirty years
of a promise she couldn't keep. And a quiet sky-watcher who sat with her one winter and told her she
was allowed to stop promising. *Not while the sky's dark,* she says. *If the sky ever wakes, come
back up and ask me again.*

Read the slates while you're up here. Each floor has one, and the tower's whole story is in them.

### After the dawn: relighting the Drownlight

**Where next? Back to the lantern room, any time after the dawn.**

Once you've beaten her and heard her story, talk to her, or to the great lamp, and she asks if
you'll open it up. (Say "Not yet" and she'll wait.) Together you lean on the great crank. The shutters fold back, the beam goes out over the
coldfog, and every buoy down the coast wakes in answer.

She gives you the **Shutter Charm**: the first shutter's own latch, which she worked off the night
she bolted it and has carried ever since. It's a keepsake charge that burns at **×2.0 on Dark-type
kin** (an ordinary lamp on anything else), and it's **never spent**: you keep it after every throw.
It isn't sold anywhere, so this one is the only one.

If you first climb the tower after the dawn, the relight follows straight on from the bout.

## The Penumbra Underways

**Where next? The two old stairs off the south of the Penumbra Ring (Starreach).**

This is the last road before the summit, and entirely optional. With all eight Gleams and
**Starreach**, two stair-mouths on islets in the Ring's starlit dark lead down. A hooded lamp with
a chalked arrow marks the west one. Below, the Hollowing keep their **last watch**: not a guard, but
a fire, so that whoever loves the Warden is close by when it ends, whichever way it ends.

You can see it before you go down. Between the two stairs, an **iron light-well** is set in the
floor of the Ring's south side. Kneel at it and you look straight down the old shaft to a small
orange fire at the bottom. Andrew mentions the watch too, once the Ring is open. The first word
of it opens **"The Last Watch"** in your JOURNAL.

It's a loop of two floors. You go in by the **west** stair and come out by the **east** stair,
back into the Ring. It never connects to the Spire.

1. **The west galleries.** Odalie, grey-haired in a long watch coat, stands just below the stair, watching the cut south. East of
   her first gallery is a **side bay** with a **Starglass Shard** behind its glow-moss. The bay's
   rubble lip is a **one-way drop** into the old cistern hall below: handy on the way down, but
   you can't climb back up it (the way back up is Odalie's cut). From the cistern hall, take the
   **west ladder** down.
2. **The Last Watch.** Bran, in the grey hood, holds the narrow way into the watch. **Old Clemency** tends the
   **watch-fire**: a free rest, and a rest point. North of the fire, a round pit gives the **view up
   the Spire's shaft**, all the way to a point of pale light at the top. (There's no way up it.)
3. **The Warm Stones.** The south-east pocket, with two ways in. Walk south from the fire along
   the **south gallery** (hood-lines, a name-slate, a water-butt and a cold brazier) and it opens
   straight onto the stones, so you can walk there and back. Or take the narrow east cut from the
   fire and hop down the **slump**, a one-way rubble drop. A watcher's tin there, marked FOR WHOEVER GOES UP,
   holds a **Lumen Drop** and a **Hearth Coal**: the last Lumen Drop on the road to the summit.
   The Warm Stones also have their own wild bed (below): the only place to find **Embralux** before
   the dawn, and the only place in the world where **JesterFull** turns up wild. Old Clemency
   mentions both, and a chalked stone by the bed has advice for the jester.

### "The Last Watch"

Let all three watchers try you (Odalie, Bran and Hesk), then go back to Old Clemency at the fire
and tell her. Hesk is on the way out, so beat him, then go back down the east ladder to the fire.
She gives you the **Star-chart: Voidburst** (Dark), the watch's own chart. It isn't sold anywhere.

Then she rings the little **watch-bell** and calls the watch in. From then on Odalie, Bran and Hesk
have left their posts (the galleries are empty where they stood) and sit together round
Clemency's fire, each with something to say.

## After the dawn

The north doesn't throw open every shutter at once. It's quieter than that.

- **Stillwater.** Two of the shore lamps stand open and lit, and their light lies on the still
  water. The punt by the jetty has its prow-lamp unhooded and burning. In Maren's house, the upstairs
  shutter is open, with a lamp in the window and a child at it. The mist thins, and everybody has
  something new to say (Elder Ashe has news of the Warden). The screens come off the hearths,
  Margery and Edwy's stove is opened up, and Keeper Wenna starts taking the shutter-wall down,
  one shutter at a time.
- **The Mourning Fields.** The sleepers wake and get to work, the scarecrow-lamps are unhooded, and
  all **five candles** burn again on the field shrine, one for each field. Tussock is awake in the
  Brook Piece. The field-tender still folds the blankets, and the first time you talk to her she
  gives you **3 more Lullwool Charms**.
- **The Drownlight** can be relit (above), and its buoys wake when it is.
- **The Underways.** The watch-lamps and the cold braziers are lit, the sleeping kin are awake,
  and the Spire's shaft is full of morning: pale light falls all the way down it onto the lower
  floor, pools in the shaft chamber and spills down toward the watch-fire. Old Clemency has a cup of tea for you.
- **The wild kin change.** The kindled and day forms lead:
