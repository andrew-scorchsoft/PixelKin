## The eight Lumenaries

Each valley's Lumenary is kept by a Lampwarden. Every one of them sets you an errand before the
bond-test, and the battle often happens somewhere other than the hall itself.

| # | Town | Lampwarden | Type | Ace | Gleam (constellation) | Lantern Gift | The errand | Purse |
|---|------|-----------|------|-----|------------------------|--------------|------------|------:|
| 1 | Tinderwick | Brisa Tallow | Ember | Hearthkit 10 | Ember (the Hearthflame) | — | Fetch the Beacon's wick-key; battle at the top of the Beacon | 600 |
| 2 | Pearlmoor Quay | Reyl Wash | Tide | Shimmral 16 | Tide (the Tideglass) | **Tidecall** | The Causeway Bell: climb the Old Light, get the rope, ring the moor-bell | 960 |
| 3 | Lowleaf Hollow | Sable Quill | Verdant | Lumournis 22 | Verdant (the Greenbough) | **Glimmerstep** | Warm the Elder Bed with a kiln-fired hearth-spore | 1,320 |
| 4 | Cinderhead Mine | Otho Grist | Stone | Ferrolith 28 | Stone (the Deepcairn) | — | Bring the vigil-lamp up from the third gallery, still lit | 1,680 |
| 5 | Galehigh Terraces | Mira Vael | Storm | Strikeaven 34 | Storm (the Stormkite) | **Updraft Kite** | Rebuild a kite, fly it at the Rising; battle on the Skyloft | 2,040 |
| 6 | Pale Vault Glacier | Ysolde Frost | Frost | Prismantus 40 | Frost (the Auroracrown) | **Emberward** | Fetch aurora-oil; light the seven brackets in the undercroft | 2,400 |
| 7 | Sunken Solarium | Lucan Pyre | Solar | Solreach 46 | Solar (the Last Sun) | **Sunsketch** | Bring up three sunmote phials; battle on the lit stage | 2,760 |
| 8 | Nightreach Observatory | Nessa Cole | Lunar | Lunarbel 52 | Lunar (the Dreaming Moon) | **Starreach** | Light the seven watch-lamps on the Astral Walk | 3,120 |

Two Gleams relight a quarter of the **Skyweave Crown**: south (Ember + Tide), east (Verdant +
Stone), north (Storm + Frost) and west (Solar + Lunar). With all four quarters whole, the Penumbra
parts and the road to the Umbral Spire opens.

Every Lumenary town also holds a festival, and each hall keeps a **sparring apprentice** who'll
battle you for wicks once its Gleam is lit (see [[making-wicks|Making Wicks]]).

## The six Lantern Gifts

| Gift | From | What it does | What it opens |
|------|------|--------------|---------------|
| **Tidecall** | Reyl Wash, Pearlmoor | Swim shallow night-water | Gullcry Rock; Pearlmoor's harbour; the far side of Saltreach Fen; Sunkbell Shallows; Tinderwick Bay and Lowtinder; the Solarium's flooded halls |
| **Glimmerstep** | Sable Quill, Lowleaf | Walk into dark caves and woods | Glowmoss Deep; Spore Grotto; Cinderhead Deep; Tideglass Cavern; Hollowfen Stillworks' inner door |
| **Updraft Kite** | Mira Vael, Galehigh | Ride thermals up terraces and across short gaps | Windward Stair's high crags; the Wind-Eye; Thunderroost; Kiln Gully; the Ashfall's Windward lookout |
| **Emberward** | Ysolde Frost, Pale Vault | Burn a path through coldfog | Hushfrost Pass; Aurora Hollow; the Coldfog Marches and Drownlight Beacon; the Hourfold |
| **Sunsketch** | Lucan Pyre, Sunken Solarium | Bloom shut night-flowers into bridges | The Sunvault Climb; Helia Vault; the Unrisen Stair |
| **Starreach** | Nessa Cole, Nightreach | Step across short voids of pure dark | The Penumbra Ring's crossings to the Umbral Spire; Starwell; the Penumbra Underways |

**The deep sea** is the one thing no Gift crosses. That takes the **Farlamp Skiff**, a key item
you earn after the dawn (see [[farlamp-isles|The Farlamp Isles]]).

## Lamplight: your lamp grows brighter

Each two Gleams make your vesperlamp brighter, and a brighter lamp lights more of the dark places.
Lamplight never blocks the main road; it only reveals optional extras.

| Tier | Gleams | Hidden things it shows |
|------|--------|------------------------|
| Ember-glow | 0–1 | — |
| Warmlight | 2 | — |
| Brightlight | 4 | A side cell in Glowmoss Deep (a Bright Balm); the moss-garden behind Lowtinder's old lamp-school (Mossmantle) |
| Starlight | 6 | An alcove on Cinderhead Deep's third floor (2 Moth-ambers); a deep nook in Tideglass Cavern (a Starglass Shard); the last ladder down to Emberroot in the Old Seams; a nook in each Coldfog marsh (a Hearth Coal and 2 Beacon Charges; a Starglass Shard) |
| Radiant | 8 | A snow-hollow in Hushfrost Pass (2 Bright Balms) |

## Getting around

| Way | How it works | When |
|-----|--------------|------|
| **The Lanternway** | Lamplit lanes from the towns to the Vesper Crossroads. Safe: no wild kin. Tinderwick's lane is walked; the Pearlmoor side runs round the bay from Saltreach Fen. | From the start; the Pearlmoor side after the Tide Gleam |
| **The Lantern-car** | A cable-car from the Crossroads winch to Lowleaf, Galehigh and Nightreach. Each line runs once that valley's Gleam is lit; the ride down always runs. | Verdant, Storm and Lunar Gleams |
| **TRAVEL** | Fast travel to any waystone town you've visited, from the pause menu | After all eight Gleams |
| **The mine shortcut** | Cinderhead Deep straight to the Crossroads | Open it from the Deep's far side |
| **The Windward drop** | A glide from the Windward Stair's high crags down to Galehigh | Reaching the high crags |
| **The Scarp road** | The rockfall between Cinderwind Scarp and Galehigh is cleared | After you reach Galehigh over the Ashfall |
| **The Tallow Lift** | Cable car between the Wick-Run's foot and crest | Once you've reached the crest |
| **The Rimeway** | High road between Pale Vault and the Coldfog Marches | Once you lift the Rime Gate from the marsh side |
| **Return to rest point** | Settings: go back to any place you've rested | Always |
