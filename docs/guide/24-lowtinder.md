From your very first morning, roof-ridges and a drowned bell-tower stand in the sea below
Tinderwick's beach. An old fisher on the sand will tell you that's where the town *used* to be.
Old Tinderwick went under "the winter the water rose", and the town moved up the bluff. Once you
hold **Tidecall**, you can swim out and see it.

## Tinderwick Bay

**How to get in:** from Tinderwick's beach, swim out past the drowned bell-tower, where the fisher
points. The way out is only from the eastern part of the beach.

The bay is a shallow reef of islets and tide-pools. The way into Lowtinder is through a single
channel off a skerry's east tip, and **Net-hand Corra** stands right there watching it.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Lamp-diver Skerry | Brineroll 17, Shimmral 18, Lumpin 19 | 304 |
| Net-hand Corra | Tidmaren 18, Brineroll 19, Shimmral 20 | 320 |

Wild kin run 17–20: Brineroll, Shimmral, Lumpin, and the rare swimmer **Tidmaren**. The bay's
outer rows are open deep water, which you'll only cross after the dawn.

## Lowtinder

The drowned old town sits on a tidal shelf: half-sunk houses, cobbles under puddles, and a loop
you walk by hopping down from roof-line to roof-line.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Eel-catcher Pip | Embrift 19, Brineroll 19, Tallowcrest 20 | 320 |
| Lampwright Hollis | Wickmoth 18, Wickmere 20, Embrift 21 | 420 |

Wild kin run 18–21. The prize is **Embrift** (Ember), which isn't wild anywhere else in
Vesperholm.

**Gran Tallow.** In the dry upper floor of the old lamp-house lives **Grandmother Ottery Tallow**,
old Tinderwick's last Lampwarden and Brisa's grandmother. She never left. Her fireside is a rest.

## "The Town Bell"

Gran Tallow's old town bell lies in the belfry pit under her cellar. She put the winch-key
somewhere safe, then forgot where safe was.

1. **The Bell Cellar.** Go down from Lowtinder into the flooded cellars. The winch-key is in the
   **winter store**, reached by a one-way rubble slump. **Old Wickman** the wick-tender (Tallowcrest
   20, Coalump 21, Wickmere 22; 440 wicks) keeps the cellar.
2. **The Belfry Pit.** Climb down to the second cellar, where the sunk bell lies. Use the winch to
   raise it. There's a **Lumen Drop** here too.
3. **Climb home.** The pit's far end is the bottom of **Tinderwick's own old well**. A rope takes
   you up into the village square. (It only goes one way.)
4. **The bell comes home.** Brisa waits at the empty bell-gallows in the square. A short ceremony
   later the bell hangs again, and the fair-chest pays out **1,500 wicks**. You can ring it from
   then on, and it rings at the dawn.

Wild kin in the cellars run 19–23.

## The moss-garden (four Gleams)

The old lamp-school in Lowtinder has a door at the back of its classroom that stays shut to a dim
lamp. Once your vesperlamp reaches **Brightlight** (four Gleams), it opens on a small moss-garden
glowing in the dark. **Mossmantle** (Light/Verdant, 24–27) lives there and nowhere else.

## "The Old Tinder Light" (after the dawn)

Gran Tallow kept the Tinder Light on the outer reef for thirty-one years, and would give her teeth
to see it once more. Nobody's boat crosses the deep, until you have the **Farlamp Skiff** (see
[[farlamp-isles|The Farlamp Isles]]).

Once you have the skiff, she asks you to row her out to the reef. There, by the old light, she asks
for a battle.

| Kin | Level | Type |

| Chandrek | 63 | Ember/Light |
| Scorchwing | 64 | Ember |
| Prismare | 64 | Tide/Light |
| Wicklord | 64 | Light/Ember |
| Embralux | 65 | Ember/Light |
| **Tideveil** (ace) | 66 | Tide/Light |

Purse: 3,960 wicks. Reward: **3 Lumen Drops**. She'll battle again whenever you row back out,
sharing the halls' practice rest (see [[making-wicks|Making Wicks]]).

- **Bring:** Tide and Stone for her Ember kin; Storm, Verdant or Dark for Prismare and Tideveil.
- **The reef's deep water** has its own wild kin (58–62), including Brinewrath, Prismare and
  Tidwraith.
