When the credits finish, **Continue** puts you back at the top of the Umbral Spire, in the first
true morning in years. Andrew's advice for this moment is the best there is: **look up.** Then walk
down.

## Dawnstead

**Where next? Down the road from the summit to Dawnstead.**

Dawnstead is a town in full daylight, and if its rooflines look familiar, they should. Its
waystone joins your TRAVEL list, so you can come back any time.

- **Còr** is off to one side of the strand, past the old tree, tending a single lamp. Talk to him
  before anything else. He isn't defeated, and he isn't punished: he has simply remembered what
  the lamp was for.
- **Fenn** is on the front, no longer hurrying you anywhere.
- **Wren** is waiting by the water, at peace, and offers one more battle "for old times". You can
  have it again whenever you like.

| Wren's rematch | Level | Type |
|----------------|-------|------|
| Glimscout | 58 | Light |
| Wickmoth | 59 | Ember |
| Frigalance | 60 | Storm/Frost |
| **Brinewrath** (ace) | 62 | Tide |

Purse: 1,488 wicks.

The town is also holding the **first-dawn festival**, the last of the game's nine.

## Day-forms: the world wakes up

The relit sky wakes the early kin of every valley in their **day-forms**. Across the whole world,
from Tinderwick's verge to the high passes, the wild tables change, and the levels jump to
**55–65**. Go back to places you know well and you'll find new things there.

- **Dawnstead's sunlit verge** has Wickmoth, Tallowpup, Hearthkit, Glimscout, **Chandrek** and
  **Wicklord**. Once you've finished the Starfall Vigils, the rare **Helixia** blooms here too.
- **The Coldfog Marches** finally lighten, and Light kin bloom where the dark used to sit.
- **In Hushfrost Pass**, the caretaker's sleeping kin is awake at her side.
- **The Ashfall Shelf** wakes **Rootwarden**, and Kiln Gully has its own day table.

## The post-game quests

### "First-Dawn Letters"

The **Waykeeper** at the Vesper Crossroads has a bundle of letters: one for Wren, one for Fenn,
and one for each of the eight Lampwardens' towns. Deliver them in any order. The four quadrant
wardens (Reyl, Otho, Ysolde and Nessa) each stamp a **Keepsake Stamp** on their reply. When every
letter is home, the Waykeeper pays **500 wicks**.

### "A Wick for Còr"

Fenn asks you to draw a wick from the **Tinderwick Beacon's lantern room**, the first made by
daylight in years, and carry it to Còr's lamp on Dawnstead's west strand.

### "The Day-form Survey"

Fenn wants three signs of day-forms for his field-journal. He points you to each spot in turn
around Dawnstead's verge: a gold wing-scale under the dawn-blooms, a shed moult by the shore, and
a warm burrow by the garden mouth. Reward: **Fenn's Journal Page**.

### Nullmajor

On the Umbral Spire's null-works floor, the null-pool that never reflected anything has started
to reflect your lamp. Something rises from it: the **Nullmajor** (Dark, lv 60), the night Còr
built, looking for a steady hand. It's a catch, and it rests for 12 won battles if it gets away.
Solar and Light hit it hard; Lunar moves do nothing.

### Lamplight finds

Your vesperlamp grows brighter with every two Gleams, and a brighter lamp shows hidden corners in
dark places. By now it's at **Radiant**, its brightest, so every one of them is open. If you
missed any on the way, the list is in [[lumenaries-and-gifts|Lumenaries & Lantern Gifts]].

## Rematches and practice bouts

- **Wren** in Dawnstead, any time (above).
- **The hall apprentices.** Every Lumenary has a sparring apprentice who'll battle you for a purse
  once that hall's Gleam is lit. They share one practice rest. See
  [[making-wicks|Making Wicks]].
- **The Vigilants and Fenn** can be fought again once the Starfall Vigils are done.
- **Gran Tallow**, **Bram Fathom** and the Undertide's **Ondine** share the halls' practice rest.

The Lampwardens themselves don't rematch: their halls' apprentices stand in for them.
