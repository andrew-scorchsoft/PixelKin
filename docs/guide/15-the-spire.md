## The Vesper Crossroads, before the climb

**Where next? North from the Vesper Crossroads, up the mountain road to the Umbral Spire.**

With the Crown whole, the Crossroads is the warmest place in the game: eight stars overhead and
every lamp lit. Before you go up:

- **Wren joins you.** As you step towards the mountain road, Wren falls in beside you to climb
  together, and hands you something Fenn sent up: the **Starlamp**, a charge that always
  catches. **Don't use it on anything else.** It's meant for the top of the Spire. (If you do spend
  it, you'll be offered another at the summit, but don't count on luck.)
- **Fenn's counsel.** Fenn is at the waystone if you'd like to hear him out one last time.
- **Rest and shop at the Vesper Crossroads Inn.** There's no shop past this door. Buy Bright
  Balms and Hearth Coals now.

### Side quests at the Crossroads

- **"Lampling's Trail"** (the Waystone kid). Follow the plaza lamps that flicker as you
  approach. At the last one sits **Lampling**, a little sentient vesperlamp kin (Light, lv 30),
  found nowhere else. Catch it.
- **"The Inn's Empty Lamps"** (the innkeeper). Four lamp-brackets want a wax token from each
  quadrant's festival. The festival folk in **Pearlmoor, Cinderhead, Pale Vault and Nightreach**
  each press one for you, **in that order**. Hang all four for the **Radiant Lamp**, a ×3.5 catch
  charge.
- **"The Waykeeper's Round."** Deliver the Waystone Kite (from Galehigh) and the fresh star-chart
  (from the Nightreach junior watcher), then walk the plaza lamps with the Waykeeper one last time.
  Reward: the **Way-lamp**, a keepsake.

## The Umbral Approach

The mountain road climbs north from the Crossroads. Three **Hollowing acolytes** keep it. They're
gentle and sad, and sure they're helping. Their kin are mostly Dark.

| Trainer | Team | Purse |
|---------|------|-------|
| Hollowing Acolyte Orla | Mothdim 50, Nightwraith 51 | 1,020 |
| Hollowing Acolyte Brenn | Cindersob 51, Whorlix 52 | 1,040 |
| Hollowing Acolyte Wynne | three kin, levels 52–53 | 1,060 |

Wild kin run 50–53 (Voidmantle, Nightwraith, Whorlix, Pyriven, Liminalux). A Starglass Shard and
a 500-wick purse are tucked off the road.

## The Penumbra Ring

The last of the dark. No wild kin live here, only memorial lamps and a camp where people simply
went quiet. The final crossings are voids of pure dark, and **Starreach** lets you step across
them. A Starglass Shard and a 600-wick purse wait off the path.

**The Underways (optional).** Two old stairs on islets off the south of the Ring lead down to
the Hollowing's **last watch**: the strongest wild Dark and Light kin before the summit, three
trainers, a rest by their fire, the last Lumen Drop, and a Star-chart from the fire's keeper once
all three watchers have tried you. It's a loop that brings you back out into
the Ring. See [[the-hollowings-country|The Hollowing's Country]].

**Starwell (optional).** A Starreach crossing off the Ring leads to a shrine of fallen starlight
and the near-legendary **Lunaveil** (Lunar, lv 54). It withdraws for 12 won battles if it gets
away. The junior watcher's chart from Nightreach names the place.

## The Umbral Spire

**Where next? Up through the Spire's four floors to the summit.**

The Spire is the dead ninth Lumenary, climbed by ladders. Wren is with you all the way.

| Floor | What's there |
|-------|--------------|
| Gatehouse | **Wren keeps the brazier going: talk to Wren for a full rest.** This is your rest point for the whole climb. Acolyte Merrin (Mothdim 52, Nullmoth 53). 2 Bright Balms. |
| Null-works | A maze floor. Acolytes Tace (Wispwane 52, Flutterwane 52, Nullmoth 53) and Ivorwen (Cindersob 53, Embergone 54). The **weave bed** here holds Skyweavelet (52–55). |
| High gallery | Acolytes Harl (Whorlix 54, Voidmantle 55) and Sefa (Liminalux 54, WispwaneNull 55). A room of cold stones nobody explains yet. A **shaft** here drops you straight back to the gatehouse fire once opened, so you can rest and come back up fast. |
| Summit | The Great Null, and Warden Còr. |

**Bring:** Solar and Light kin (both hit Dark hard). **Avoid:** leaning on Lunar moves, which do
nothing to Dark kin, and on Light/Lunar kin, which Dark hits hard.

## The summit

### Warden Còr

Còr stands before the **Great Null**, his lantern built to hold no light, trained on the
**Keystar**. He doesn't gloat. He explains, one last time and very gently, that he only wants the
grieving to stop. Then you battle.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Team** | Six kin, almost all Dark, levels 53–56 |
| **Notables** | Stillwarden (Frost/Dark), Liminalux (Dark/Light), Voidmantle (Dark), Embergone (Dark/Ember) |
| **Ace** | **Nullmajor**, Dark, lv 56 |
| **Purse** | 6,720 wicks |

- **Bring:** Solar (four times over on Stillwarden), Light, and Tide for Embergone.
- **Level:** 54–56. Rest at Wren's fire first.

Beating his kin doesn't defeat Còr. It earns you the right to answer him, and the answer isn't an
argument. You **out-remember** him: every relit constellation, every festival, every kin that
chose to walk with you.

### The Keystar

The **Keylumen**, the Keystar's living heart (Light, lv 55), wakes from the old lantern-housing.
This is a catch, not a fight to the finish. Raise the **Starlamp**, Fenn's forty-year-old gift,
and it comes to you. If you've spent the Starlamp, you're offered another; and if the Keylumen
gets away, it never withdraws, so you can always try again.

Then the Keystar catches, light runs back along the whole Skyweave, and for the first time in
years, **dawn breaks**.

## After the credits

When the credits finish you return to the title screen. **Continue** puts you back at the summit
in the morning, and the road down to **Dawnstead** is open.

That's the end of the main journey, and the start of a lot more. See
[[dawnstead|Dawnstead & the Morning After]].
