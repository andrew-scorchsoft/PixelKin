PixelKin plays like the handhelds it remembers: a d-pad, two face buttons and a START button.
It runs in your browser on a computer or a phone, and the same journey works with either.

## The controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch (on-screen pad) |
|--------|----------|-----------------------|
| Walk | Arrow keys or W / A / S / D | D-pad |
| Confirm, talk, read a sign | Z, Space or Enter | A |
| Cancel, back | X or Backspace | B |
| Run | Hold X while walking | Hold B while walking |
| Pause menu | Esc | START |

**Running.** Hold Cancel while you walk to run. If you'd rather run all the time, set
**Pace: Always run** in Settings.

**Doors.** Walk into a doorway and you go in; no button needed. A locked door tells you why it's
locked when you bump into it.

**Counters and beds.** Facing a shop counter reaches the keeper standing behind it, so you don't
need to walk round. A bed or other big piece of furniture answers from any side.

**Wild kin on the map.** Some kin wander the overworld for colour. Walk up next to one and press
Confirm to say hello: it turns, makes its cry and has a little something to say. These are only
greetings, never battles, and saying hello marks the kin as seen in your Register.

### On a phone

The **Device** shell (the default) frames the game like a handheld, with the pad below the screen
in portrait or down the sides in landscape. Turn your phone sideways for the biggest picture.

- **Fullscreen.** In landscape on Android, the first button press asks for fullscreen, and a small
  toggle sits in the corner. iPhone browsers don't allow fullscreen for games.
- **Add to Home Screen.** Install PixelKin from your browser's share menu and it opens with no
  address bar. On iPhone this is the only way to get the whole screen.
- **Button size.** Settings → **Controls** cycles Size 1, 2, 3 and Hidden.
- **Lost your buttons?** If you hide the controls or pick the Plain shell on a touch screen, a
  **Show controls** button appears so you can always get them back.

## The pause menu

Press START (or Esc) in the world. Under the menu, a **NEXT** panel tells you the current step of
the main journey in a line. When you're stuck, look there first.

| Entry | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| RESUME | Back to the game. |
| KIN | Your party of up to six. View a kin's stats and moves, or reorder them (the top kin leads in battle). |
| REGISTER | Your record of every kin you've seen and caught, with where each one can be found. |
| GLEAMS | Your relit constellations, laid out by region. It appears once you hold the first one, and the empty spaces show where the rest are waiting. |
| JOURNAL | Every named quest you've started, with your progress. "The Wayfaring" tracks the main journey. |
| MAP | The region map, with a marker where you're standing. |
| TRAVEL | Fast travel between the towns you've visited. It appears late in the journey, once the eight Gleams are relit. |
| HEARTH | Your kin storage. Move kin between your lamp and the Hearth. |
| ITEMS | Your pack. Use balms, toggle key items, read Star-charts. |
| LORE | A small codex of words and places. New entries fill in as you travel. |
| CHARTS | A gallery of painted views of the places you've discovered. |
| SAVE | Save your journey. |
| SETTINGS | All the options below. |

## Saving

Pick **SAVE** from the pause menu. There are several save slots; the title screen's
**CONTINUE** lists them. The game also keeps your progress between saves (a small SAVED glyph
flashes in the corner when it does), but a manual save is the one to trust.

Your journey lives in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data, or switch
browser or device, it won't come with you unless you've kept a copy as a file. There are three
ways to do that:

1. **After every manual save**, the game offers to keep a copy as a file. Say yes now and then.
2. **Settings → Backup / restore** has *Export a copy* and *Import a copy*.
3. On the title screen, **LOAD FILE** opens a save file and lets you pick which slot to put it in.

When you continue an older save after the game has been updated, a short "the world has changed"
note tells you what's new.

## Settings

Open Settings from the pause menu or the title screen.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Return to rest point | *(In the world only.)* Takes you back to any place you've rested. Use it if you're ever stuck somewhere. |
| Shell | Device, Overlay or Plain: how the game is framed on screen. |
| Controls | On-screen button size (1, 2, 3) or Hidden. |
| Sound | Mute everything. |
| Volume | Overall volume. On a phone in portrait, the slider at the top of the deck does the same. |
| Pace | Walk, or Always run. |
| Text | Cosy, Brisk or Instant text speed. |
| Battle | Cosy or Swift battle animations. |
| Trainer talk | *Press A* waits for you on a trainer's lines; *Auto* moves on by itself. |
| Music | Music level (Off, Low, Mid, Full). |
| Soundtrack | **Remastered** (the default) or **Classic** (the chiptune scores). Battles always use the chiptune versions. |
| Effects | Sound-effect level. |
| Cannot die | If your whole party faints, they're healed where they stand. You lose nothing, and the battle simply waits for you to try again. |
| EXP share | On by default. Kin that fought share in the experience, and the rest of the party gets a smaller share. |
| Ambient motion | Turns the drifting motes, mist and wandering kin on or off. |
| Backup / restore | Export or import a save file. |

### Return to rest point

Every time you rest (at an inn, a home, a fireside), the game remembers that place. **Settings →
Return to rest point** lists them all, newest first, and takes you to any of them. It's never
locked, so if you ever wander somewhere you can't get out of, this is your way home.

## Getting help in the world

- **The NEXT panel** in the pause menu always names the next main-journey step.
- **Andrew**, who leans on a fence in every rim town, will tell you where to go next and roughly
  how to get there. If you're lost, find him.
- **The Wickstead Lamp-school**, west of Tinderwick through Duskapple Orchard, has a teacher for
  each part of the game: types, catching, the seven troubles (status conditions), kindling, the
  Hearth, resting, wicks and Lantern Gifts. It's optional and safe, and you can visit before you
  even have a kin.
