The Music of Vesperholm

Every place in PixelKin has its own tune — and all of them grow out of one melody, the lullaby of Tinderwick, the town where your Wayfaring begins. Listen to each one as the original chip render or the remastered score, and download any track you like.

The remastered score is the default in the game. Each track opens with a short intro that plays once as you arrive, then settles into a loop that never repeats the intro, so the music carries on as you walk between places. Prefer the original sound? Switch Soundtrack to Classic in the game's Settings.

Play runs a track as the game does: intro once, then the loop. Loop only skips the intro, Loop point starts a few seconds before the track wraps so you can hear the join, and Full once plays the file straight through to its end.
